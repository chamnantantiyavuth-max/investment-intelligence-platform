# Foundation Amendment v0.2

## Status

**Founder-approved**

## Approval Statement

The Founder approved all Proposed Foundation Amendments v0.2 and authorized the candidate v0.2 governance overlay to be incorporated into the Investment Intelligence Platform repository.

## Purpose

Harden agent governance, change control, verification, security, and approval semantics before Hermes begins Project Definition work.

## Approved Amendments

1. Rename `Controlled Execution Universe` to `Controlled Screening Universe`.
2. Preserve Alpha Momentum as the selected first vertical slice while treating its effectiveness as a testable hypothesis.
3. Clarify that Approved Theme means approved for tracking and strategy context—not approved investment.
4. Permit controlled evidence quarantine or deletion with tombstones and lineage impact.
5. Require audit records for material lifecycle and governance transitions.
6. Strengthen authority hierarchy and explicit approval semantics.
7. Define material changes and required proposals.
8. Treat all external content as untrusted input.
9. Adopt a Verification Doctrine.
10. Separate tooling experiments from product hypotheses.
