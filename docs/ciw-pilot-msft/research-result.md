## 2. Dimension Summaries (each linked to evidence — full lineage in draft §5/§6)

### Business Quality — HIGH (strong evidence)
- FY26 revenue $331.8B (+18%, +16% CC); Microsoft Cloud $214.4B (+27%); commercial RPO $678B (+84% total, +25% ex-OpenAI), 2.3-yr weighted duration, ~30% within 12 months. Segment revenue: PBP $139,996M, IC $137,791M, MPC $54,052M. [SRC-001 Note 12/18; SRC-003a; SRC-003t]
- Operating income $155.2B (+21%); operating margin 46.8% (FY26). [SRC-XBR computed]
- **Limitation:** market rank/share not established from cited primary sources (N1 disposition); moat mechanism evidence is issuer-reported indicators at initial depth (F4).

### Moat — WIDE / DEEP / WIDENING (Phase 8 canonical, consumed not re-derived; CIW adds initial-depth primary-source support)
- Issuer-reported indicators consistent with the moat hypothesis: switching costs (M365 installed base, RPO 2.3yr, Copilot enterprise deployment ~75% QoQ growth), network effects (GitHub 225M users, 90% Fortune 500; platform ecosystems), intangible assets (R&D $35.6B, IP). [SRC-003t; SRC-001 Item 1]
- Scale economies (datacenters) explicitly distinguished from network effects (F4). "Widening" is an initial-depth HYPOTHESIS at the platform layer, tested at the frontier-AI layer (model-choice architecture reduces single-model dependence). Missing disconfirming evidence (churn/migration/competitor data) identified as gap. [draft Module C]
- Canonical Phase 8 classification is NOT re-derived or altered by CIW (CRR §2).

### Balance Sheet — STRONG with material fixed commitments
- Cash + ST investments $76.8B; total debt $40.3B; equity $442.4B; net cash. OCF $182.9B (FY26). No near-term refinancing risk on current evidence. [SRC-001 balance sheet]
- **Commitment intensity (F5):** contractual obligations $743.8B total (FY27 $241.9B): lease payments incl. imputed interest $443.5B, purchase commitments $194.1B, construction $34.6B, debt principal+interest $71.7B. Not-yet-commenced leases $329.1B (FY27–FY33, primarily datacenter; overlap caution — not double-counted). Stressed-level OCF unquantified at initial depth (F7). [SRC-001 MD&A; Note 13]

### Management — STRONG (evidence-linked)
- Nadella CEO since 2014; FY26 record; candid guidance-miss disclosure (Q4 discrete items, net $0.27 EPS benefit, fully explained). Non-GAAP usage reasonable (OpenAI investment impacts excluded with full reconciliation). [SRC-003a; SRC-003t]
- Compensation: Nadella FY25 total $96.5M, 87% stock-dominant, PSAs with 3-yr relative TSR; no employment contracts; no change-in-control payments; clawback policy. Combined Chairman/CEO (mitigated by Lead Independent Director). [SRC-004]
- OpenAI related-party complexity: ~25% as-converted equity, $13.0B commitments ($11.9B funded), HLBV accounting; FY26 related-party revenue $24.1B (7.3% of total). [SRC-001 Note]

## 4. Unresolved Questions (explicit)

1. **The central question:** does the $115.9B FY26 → ~$175B CY26 capex build earn >WACC? (Owner-earnings spread $56B–$134B; 60% maintenance split unsupported at initial depth; falsifier methodology published — needs FY27–FY29 data.)
2. OpenAI-independent growth durability: RPO +25% ex-OpenAI vs +84% total; ~90% of Cloud revenue ex-frontier-model (subgroup growth rate NOT disclosed).
3. Moat mechanism verification at customer/competitor level (churn, migration, win/loss) — missing at initial depth.
4. Regulatory outcomes: EU/US antitrust, IDPC LinkedIn appeal ($553M accrued; ~$400M reasonably possible beyond).
5. Accounting-optics monitoring: FY27 useful-life extension (15→25yr) + lease reclassification effects on reported capex/margins.
6. Commitment-stack precision: exact non-overlap between $443.5B lease payments and $329.1B not-yet-commenced leases (needs underlying schedules).
