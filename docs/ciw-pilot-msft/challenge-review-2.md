## 3. DCF, Reverse DCF, and Margin-of-Safety Reperformance

### 3.1 Formula used

For OE0 $129.633B, explicit years 1–5:

`PV = Σ[OE0(1+g)^t/(1+r)^t] + [OE5(1+g_terminal)/(r−g_terminal)]/(1+r)^5`.

This is the formula implied by the draft's scenario values.

| Case | Inputs (growth / r / terminal g) | Independent total | Independent per share | Draft | Review |

**Does the conclusion follow?** Yes, narrowly: $464.72 is 43% above Base IV when measured as price premium to IV and 5.7% below Bull IV, so “above Base, near Bull” follows from this table. It does **not** validate the unsupported starting OE, and it does not imply a 9–12% expected return.
