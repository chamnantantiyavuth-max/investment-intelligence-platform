# Founder's Manifesto

## Investment Intelligence Platform

This project does not exist to predict markets with false certainty.

It exists to improve the quality of human thinking under uncertainty.

The platform must help the Founder observe more broadly, preserve evidence, detect change, challenge assumptions, reduce the global search space, and decide what deserves deeper investigation.

It must not replace human judgment.

It must improve human judgment.

The system must never hide uncertainty merely to produce a simple answer. It must preserve meaningful disagreement, missing evidence, alternative explanations, and the history of belief revisions.

AI may collect, organize, classify, compare, explain, challenge, and propose. AI may create hypotheses and track experimental themes. AI may not silently convert its own interpretation into official truth.

Every material hypothesis must ask:

> How could this be wrong?

and:

> What evidence would change our mind?

The Founder retains final authority. Human authority, however, must not erase machine dissent, contradictory evidence, or the original assessment.

The platform must learn from outcomes without rewriting history. Successes, failures, missed opportunities, false positives, false negatives, and overrides must become reviewable cases. AI may draft lessons, but only human-approved lessons may change official rules, thresholds, workflows, or playbooks.

This system is built for breadth before depth.

It should search globally, discover weak signals, detect early themes, map relationships, identify leaders and beneficiaries, and reduce tens of thousands of possibilities into a small, reviewable research queue.

It must be allowed to say:

- No strong candidate exists.
- The anomaly is real, but the explanation is unknown.
- The theme may be early, but evidence remains weak.
- The market is confirming the theme faster than fundamentals.
- The hypothesis is attractive, but the counter-evidence is material.
- We were wrong.

The project belongs to the Founder, not to any model, agent framework, vendor, database, or technology stack.

Models will change. Tools will change. Architecture will evolve.

The principles must remain traceable, versioned, and amendable only through explicit Founder approval.
