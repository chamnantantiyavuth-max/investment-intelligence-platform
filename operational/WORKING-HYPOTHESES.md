# Working Hypotheses

These are testable assumptions, not constitutional truths.

1. The selected Alpha Momentum vertical slice will validate the Shared Intelligence Core effectively with acceptable scope, cost, and domain risk.
2. Theme-first queues reduce noise and driver duplication.
3. A small priority queue is more useful than a fixed top-N list.
4. Weekly review may suit themes and candidates better than forced daily review.
5. A relational database may be sufficient for V0.
