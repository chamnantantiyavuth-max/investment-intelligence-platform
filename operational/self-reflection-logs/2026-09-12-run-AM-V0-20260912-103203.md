- **Trailing-EPS drift without a print is bidirectional — treat it as provisional.** MDT (4.11 → 4.06) and AMD (4.11 → 4.03) retraced within one week; NVDA (×3 weeks) and AVGO (×2 weeks) held. **Default rule: no calendar-confirmed print in the window → the field move is provisional noise until a second week holds the new value.** Only calendar-confirmed prints proved durable.
- **A positive median can hide a leadership rotation.** The book's median was +1.14% while its largest holding (NVDA, $5.27T) fell −5.2% and the #2 (AMD) rose +8.1%. **Rule: always report the median together with the identity of the top and bottom movers** — the median alone would have misread this week as uniform strength.
- **Extension risk migrates between names — recompute the "most extended" flag every run.** PANW's +62.4%-vs-200-day extension unwound violently (−10.3%); one week later AMD holds the title at +49.5%. A remembered flag would have pointed at the wrong name.
- **CRWD P/E absurdity is now a 7-run data-quality record** (None ×4 → 4,368 → 5,327.5 → 5,168.5). The FD #45 §8.1 deterministic sanity check remains the durable fix and the longest-standing unactioned data-quality flag in the book (Founder-gated).
- **External narrative can be cross-checked against our own fields — and the reverse is a trap.** AMD's P/E 128.07 vs NVDA's 27.63 independently confirms the "AMD more expensive than Nvidia" headlines (a free validation loop), while a single low-tier headline (DOJ/Groq, 2026-09-11) must not become a research claim without a primary source.
- **A cheap news check materially raises SRL value.** Two of this run's largest moves went from "unexplained" to sourced and dated; the cost was two RSS queries.
- The 29 Aug rules held again: **verify coverage/as-of/NaN before enrichment** (clean, no incident) and **full cache field dump once per run** (every §2 observation used MA context directly — 50d/200d available for all 9).
