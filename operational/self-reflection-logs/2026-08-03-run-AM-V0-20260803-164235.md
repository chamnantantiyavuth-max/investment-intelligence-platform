## 5. Lessons

- **Weekly cadence + real EOD = the minimum viable monitoring loop** for V0: a 7–9 day interval produces visible price/P/E structure movement on 5/9 candidates without noise. The 24-hour cache freshness in `source_adapter.py` is the right default for weekly runs (cache from Jul 25 was auto-refreshed on Aug 3).
- **SRL skeletons are necessary but insufficient:** the auto-generator catches run context and fixture-based surprises but cannot do cross-run price analysis. Enrichment against the prior enriched baseline is what makes the learning loop compound. Until Phase 6B thesis-diff lands, the weekly driver should always pair the skeleton with a manual delta table.
- **P/E ratios are first-order checks:** P/E movement decoupled from price movement (FSLR) is a cheap, high-signal integrity check on the adapter's fundamental fields.
- **A flagged gap with no owner becomes a repeat finding:** gaps need a disposition (fixed / deferred-with-ID), not just documentation.
