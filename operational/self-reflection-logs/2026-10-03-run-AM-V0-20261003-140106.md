## 5. Lessons

- **State the window length and normalize the median.** This run's baseline is 14 days (missing 26 Sep fire): median +9.9% ⇒ ≈**+4.8%/week**. A book median is only comparable across runs when the window length is stated — put the window length in the header next to the median, always.
- **"A trailing-EPS field held after a print" is a time-bounded statement, not a durability guarantee.** NVDA (7.90→7.91 held 4 weeks, then →8.02) and AVGO (7.83 held 3 weeks, then →8.10) both recomputed with no print. Only a calendar-confirmed print is an event; everything between prints is field recomputation.
- **In a broad melt-up, separate market beta from idiosyncratic signal before attributing anything.** 6/9 up with the Nasdaq at a record close (Reuters/BBC); the book's dispersion this window is mostly the AI/semis tape. The two *idiosyncratic* reads are the movers **against** the tape — MDT (−6.2%) and FSLR (−10.9%).
- **Calendar-first grounding is cheap and it again flipped the framing.** One `yfinance.calendar` call for all 9 tickers established that **no** name had a print in the window, converting a whole batch of eps-field moves from potential fundamental events into definite field defects.
- **A single weekly close is not a floor.** MDT's "stabilized" read (one close) was reversed next window. Require ≥2 consecutive closes before declaring a level "held" (the same discipline the field-hold rule already uses).
- **Report median + mean + spread together** (median +9.9%, mean +5.21%, spread 24.5pts) — the mean/median gap remains the cheapest diagnostic for "a few big winners dominate".
- **Keep the sector-pair check.** TH-030's pair median (+12.25%) again exceeded the book median; a 2-name theme carries a real sector signal that the book median alone would hide.
- The 29 Aug rules held again: **verify coverage / as-of / NaN before enrichment** (clean) and **dump all cache fields once per run** (50d/200d MA context available for all 9).
