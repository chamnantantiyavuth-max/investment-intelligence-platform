# Domain Glossary

## Evidence
Traceable information or observation.

## Claim
A statement that may remain unverified.

## Weak Signal
A potentially meaningful observation with insufficient evidence.

## Unexplained Anomaly
Detected change without a credible explanation.

## Theme Hypothesis
A provisional explanation connecting drivers, evidence, and entities.

## Experimental Theme
An autonomously tracked theme that cannot affect official strategy outputs.

## Approved Theme
A human-approved theme eligible for official tracking and strategy context.

Approval is not a buy recommendation, investment endorsement, capital-allocation decision, or declaration that the hypothesis is proven.

## Lifecycle
The theme's market or economic development stage.

## Governance State
The theme's approval and operational status.

## Candidate Quality
Quality of the company or asset, excluding entry timing.

## Theme Quality
Strength, health, evidence progression, breadth, leadership, and crowding of the related theme.

## Entry Readiness
Technical readiness and extension risk.

## Data Confidence
Freshness, completeness, reliability, and conflict state.

## Human Override
A human decision that differs from system assessment while preserving machine dissent.

## Approved Knowledge
A reviewed lesson eligible to inform official rules or playbooks.
