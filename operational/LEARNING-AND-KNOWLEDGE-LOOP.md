# Learning and Knowledge Loop

Evidence  
→ Hypothesis  
→ Theme  
→ Candidate  
→ Research  
→ Human Decision  
→ Outcome  
→ Postmortem  
→ AI Lesson Draft  
→ Human Review  
→ Approved Lesson  
→ Rule, Skill, or Playbook Proposal

## Rules

- AI lessons are not official knowledge.
- Approved lessons link back to cases and evidence.
- Rule changes are versioned.
- History is not silently rewritten.
- Successes and failures both deserve postmortems when material.

## Knowledge Layers

### Application
Structured source of truth.

### Obsidian / NotebookLM
Narrative case studies, playbooks, patterns, and reflection.
