# AI Governance

## AI May

- collect and structure public information;
- extract entities and relationships;
- detect anomalies;
- suggest hypotheses;
- summarize evidence;
- identify counter-evidence;
- propose tests;
- draft postmortems;
- propose lessons and rule changes.

## AI May Not

- approve official themes;
- change official rules or thresholds automatically;
- execute trades;
- allocate capital;
- hide conflicts;
- convert unsupported claims into facts;
- rewrite history;
- invent missing business rules.

## AI Output Lineage

Material AI-derived fields must record model, version, workflow or prompt version, timestamp, input evidence, confidence, and review status.
