**Design rule:** workflow stages are MANDATORY; the ANALYTICAL CONTENT within each stage is FREE-FORM (Analytical Freedom Doctrine, FD #64 item 7 — specs/checklists are NOT auto-loaded into first passes). This is a process contract, NOT a content template, NOT a scorecard.

## Purpose

Make the proven deep-research workflow reusable for ANY Full Company Research
Mandate — without re-inventing the pipeline each time and without constraining
what the analyst thinks or writes.

> ChatGPT FIT-GAP WP3: "เอาความสำเร็จของ Apple FD #87 มาทำเป็น reusable research
> workflow แต่ยังคง free-form analytical freedom" — the research organization
> itself is NOT broken; what was missing was a standing contract for the full
> research cell. (11 Aug 2026)

## When this contract applies

- Full Company Deep Research Mandate (RM-####) — e.g. multi-dimension deep
  analysis, first FO-universe deep analysis, moat durability deep-dive.
- Triggered ONLY via CoS triage → RM. Never auto-triggered by a scanner
  (FD #88 firewall: evidence blocks never enter research capacity by themselves).
- Quality & Asymmetry Discovery (WP2) and Equity Inflection (FD #88) BOTH feed
  into this same contract — one research engine, many idea sources.

## Mandatory stages (in order)

```
1.  RESEARCH MANDATE (RM-####) — scoped question, approved at CoS triage
2.  ANTI-ANCHORING — independent first passes BEFORE any shared essay
    (N isolated views; specs NOT auto-loaded — FD #64 item 7)
3.  EVIDENCE BUILD — primary sources only (SEC EDGAR filings, transcripts,
    XBRL); every material figure date-stamped + sourced (FD #58 point-in-time)
4.  MAIN ESSAY — free-form analytical essay from the evidence
5.  CROSS-EXAMINATION — challenger MUST-FIX findings vs the essay
6.  CRO OPPOSING THESIS — independent dissent (via Sol Medium, FD #73 routing)
7.  AUDIT → RE-AUDIT — corrections applied; originals preserved (Constitution
    §23.9 CORRECTIONS-RECORD); verdict recorded
8.  FACTS LOCKED — editor may rewrite/reorder/shorten/explain but NEVER change
    figures/accessions/dates/uncertainty/material dissent/conclusions
    (FD #94 THAI-RESEARCH-EDITORIAL-STANDARD)
9.  IC SECRETARY EDITORIAL SYNTHESIS — fresh Thai composition (FD #92),
    internal governance jargon stripped (FD #94 publication firewall)
10. FOUNDER GATE — Option A publish / Option B publish with dissent / rework
11. PUBLISH — reports/<slug>.md + CRO companion, series cross-linked
```

## Hard boundaries (binding)

- **Analytical freedom:** the contract mandates STAGES, never conclusions,
  scores, or forced structure inside an essay. No checklist disease.
- **Anti-anchoring:** the first pass MUST be isolated — one analyst does not
  see another's draft before forming a view (RM-2026-0004 precedent).
- **Dissent preserved:** CRO opposing thesis is published as a companion, never
  folded into the main essay (§23.9).
- **Facts Locked (FD #94):** editorial freedom stops at figures/accessions/
  dates/uncertainty/material dissent/conclusions.
- **Publication firewall (FD #94):** no RM/ORG/FD/spec/§/workspace/audit-status/
  portfolio-blind/SRC labels in published article bodies — provenance lives in
  git + research/ + evidence/ + Audit Center.
- **Portfolio-blind (Constitution §23.8.1):** research never receives portfolio
  context. Advisory only — no buy/sell/allocate.
- **No invented thresholds (FD #53):** any numeric rule used in research must
  trace to an approved spec or Founder-approved value.

## Roles

- Lead Analyst (role 03/05/04 per asset class — e.g. Equity Alpha Analyst for
  companies, Commodity Product Analyst for commodities, Macro Strategist for macro)
- Challenger / Cross-examiner (independent view)
- CRO (role 07) — opposing thesis via Sol Medium (FD #73)
- Internal Auditor / Red Team (role 10) — audit + re-audit
- IC Secretary (role 02) — editorial synthesis + FACTS LOCKED gate
- Founder — final gate (authority: publish / publish with dissent / rework)

## Verification

- Contract stages completed → artifacts present in workspace
- Audit verdict recorded (CLEAN / CLEAN WITH MINORS / REMAINS-BLOCKED)
- FACTS LOCKED token-preservation check (numbers/accessions/dates preserved)
- Jargon sweep 0 on published body (FD #94)
- /library + article page browser-verified
