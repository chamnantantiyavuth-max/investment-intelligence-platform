## 2. Cross-Cutting Runtime Contracts (apply to every role)

1. **§23.5 contract completeness** — each PRINCIPAL.md must state: purpose, authority boundary, permitted evidence, prohibited actions, input/output contracts, deterministic dependencies, provenance, validation, failure behavior, escalation, review requirements. The pack covers ~7/11; **missing: deterministic dependencies, provenance, failure behavior (F-18), review requirements** — added in the rewrite.
2. **§23.7 failure behavior** — default per role: retry → queue → incomplete-with-review (never fabricate, never degrade deterministic records). Recordkeeping functions remain operable without the role.
3. **Provenance/lineage (Constitution §23.5)** — every material contribution records which profile/role produced it; multi-role artifacts record where roles agreed/disagreed.
4. **Portfolio-blind (Constitution §23.8.1)** — no role or Assistant receives holdings/positions/cost basis/transactions/account data. Violation = Governance Hold trigger + immediate escalation.
5. **Model-routing discipline (F-14)** — Flash-to-Flash "independent" review is advisory; material Independent Challenge, governance audit, Phase 2R, LLM Council → Sol Medium (openai-codex, fallback luna) per FD-HERMES-007/project-workflow §0C.
6. **No silent promotion** — org artifacts never change canonical state (Theme Approval Status/Monitoring Status, Thesis Lifecycle, Research State, Candidate state, Investment state). Only Founder + recorded FD.
7. **Assistant boundary (pack §9, retained)** — Assistants gather/extract/organize/draft-labeled; never approve, certify, sign, resolve conflicts, change governance state, clear Holds, or delegate recursively without explicit permission; every substantive output begins `ASSISTANT DRAFT — PRINCIPAL REVIEW REQUIRED`.

## 3. Role Overlap and Conflict Notes (from fit-gap F-09/F-10/F-11)

- **Equity Alpha ↔ CIW (F-09):** the role may *consume* published CIW results (e.g., `research-result.md` v1) and perform bounded research on them; it may NOT open new CIW-path research or automation without a named FD superseding FD #44 (CIW paused until Q1-FY27).
- **CRO ↔ Independent Challenge (F-10):** CRO is the named operator of the existing challenge function for org workflows; the function's independence requirements (QUALITY-GATES §1) still bind — the CRO never challenges in the author's session.
- **Internal Auditor ↔ Sol Medium (F-11):** audit execution is delegated; the profile orchestrates. This preserves FD-HERMES-007 (no Parent self-audit) and model-family independence.
- **Data Steward ↔ Shared Core (canonical ownership):** the capability belongs to Shared Core; the role is the accountable human-reviewable operator, not a new owner of domain truth.
