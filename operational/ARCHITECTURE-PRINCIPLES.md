# Architecture Principles

1. Modular monolith first.
2. Shared intelligence is separate from strategy-specific logic.
3. Raw evidence is immutable.
4. Material transformations are versioned.
5. Point-in-time correctness matters.
6. Outputs must be explainable without reading source code.
7. Do not require a graph database prematurely.
8. AI providers and prompts must be replaceable.
9. Reproducibility outranks convenience.
10. Build complete vertical slices.
11. Do not introduce infrastructure without a current requirement.
12. Historical state changes are append-only or otherwise auditable.
