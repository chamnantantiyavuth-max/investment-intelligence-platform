# Tooling Experiments

## Status

Operational experiment register. Tooling choices are not product architecture.

## TE-001 — Hermes + DeepSeek V4 Pro

### Hypothesis

Hermes with DeepSeek V4 Pro may provide a cost-effective primary development environment when verification, repository controls, and project-specific governance are strong.

### Current Status

Approved for controlled evaluation, not permanently adopted.

### Evaluation Criteria

- cost per verified task;
- adherence to the Constitution and `AGENTS.md`;
- plan quality;
- scope discipline;
- verification quality;
- regression rate;
- preservation of domain semantics;
- repository-boundary and security compliance;
- required Founder intervention;
- ease of provider replacement.

### Architectural Constraint

No domain model, data contract, history format, or official workflow may become inseparable from Hermes or DeepSeek.

### Exit Condition

Retain, revise, or replace the stack based on observed project performance rather than benchmark reputation alone.
