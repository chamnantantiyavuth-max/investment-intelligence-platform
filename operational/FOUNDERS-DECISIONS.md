# Founder's Decisions

These decisions are approved constitutional constraints.

1. Strategy Control Center is the primary entrance.
2. Alpha Momentum is the first vertical slice.
3. Theme Intelligence belongs to the Shared Intelligence Core.
4. Evidence is a foundational domain object.
5. Theme discovery is hybrid: top-down, bottom-up, event-driven, and change-driven.
6. AI may create Experimental Themes; only humans approve official themes.
7. Weak Signal Inbox separates Unexplained Anomalies from Theme Hypotheses.
8. Research Queue is Theme-first.
9. Queue capacity is adaptive and may return zero candidates.
10. Global evidence feeds a controlled V0 universe of US-listed common stocks and suitable ADRs.
11. Conflicting evidence remains visible.
12. Every material hypothesis must attempt to disprove itself.
13. Human overrides preserve machine dissent.
14. AI may propose learning but may not enforce rule changes.
15. The application is the structured source of truth; Obsidian and NotebookLM are narrative learning layers.
