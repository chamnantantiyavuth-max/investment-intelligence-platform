# Evidence Doctrine

## Required Separations

- raw source;
- fact;
- claim;
- normalized fact;
- derived metric;
- statistical signal;
- AI extraction;
- AI classification;
- hypothesis;
- human judgment;
- decision;
- outcome;
- lesson.

## Source Independence

Syndicated copies do not count as independent confirmation.

## Evidence Progression

Structural Signal  
→ Operational Action  
→ Fundamental Confirmation  
→ Market Confirmation  
→ Broad Adoption / Crowding

## Aging

Evidence is retained. Current relevance changes.

Raw evidence must not be silently edited in place. When legal, privacy, licensing, security, corruption, or retention requirements demand removal, the content may be quarantined or deleted through a controlled process that records:

- a tombstone;
- the reason;
- the authorizer;
- the timestamp;
- affected lineage;
- downstream invalidation and reprocessing requirements.

## Three-Year Default

Unsupported narrative or intention with no measurable action within three years becomes stale by default, unless a documented long-cycle exception is approved.

## Point-in-Time Data in Reference Works (FD #58 — Must Rule)

Books, industry handbooks, and reference materials are static historical artifacts. Several in `docs/Books/` were written decades ago (e.g., Klarman 1991, Natenberg 1994, Marx 1867, Investopedia handbooks 2015).

- **All quantitative figures cited in reference works — production costs, prices, financial figures, ratios, market data, industry statistics — are point-in-time values valid only at the work's publication date. They MUST NOT be treated as current.**
- Any such figure must be updated or re-verified against a current source (company filings, current market data, current industry statistics) before it may inform research direction, theme analysis, evidence, or any pipeline output.
- The durable value of reference works is structural and conceptual: supply-chain structure, what to measure, where to look, Porter's 5 Forces, valuation frameworks. Their numbers expire; their frameworks do not.
- Applies to all reference books and handbooks in `docs/Books/`, the Obsidian vault, and any future industry-outlook reference layer built from them.

## Contradictions

Contradicting evidence remains visible and is never averaged away merely for presentation simplicity.
