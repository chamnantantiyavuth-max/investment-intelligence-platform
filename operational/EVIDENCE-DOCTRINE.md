# Evidence Doctrine

## Required Separations

- raw source;
- fact;
- claim;
- normalized fact;
- derived metric;
- statistical signal;
- AI extraction;
- AI classification;
- hypothesis;
- human judgment;
- decision;
- outcome;
- lesson.

## Source Independence

Syndicated copies do not count as independent confirmation.

## Evidence Progression

Structural Signal  
→ Operational Action  
→ Fundamental Confirmation  
→ Market Confirmation  
→ Broad Adoption / Crowding

## Aging

Evidence is retained. Current relevance changes.

Raw evidence must not be silently edited in place. When legal, privacy, licensing, security, corruption, or retention requirements demand removal, the content may be quarantined or deleted through a controlled process that records:

- a tombstone;
- the reason;
- the authorizer;
- the timestamp;
- affected lineage;
- downstream invalidation and reprocessing requirements.

## Three-Year Default

Unsupported narrative or intention with no measurable action within three years becomes stale by default, unless a documented long-cycle exception is approved.

## Contradictions

Contradicting evidence remains visible and is never averaged away merely for presentation simplicity.
