# Human Review and Override

The Founder has final authority.

A Human Override records:

- system assessment;
- machine dissent;
- human decision;
- rationale;
- unresolved counter-evidence;
- required confirmation;
- reassessment condition or date;
- outcome.

Overrides do not rewrite prior system states.

The platform may later analyze override patterns to improve the decision process, but not to judge the Founder.
