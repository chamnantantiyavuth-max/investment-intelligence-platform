# Scope and Non-Scope

## In Scope

- public evidence collection;
- provenance, source independence, and discovery provenance;
- anomaly, dislocation, and quality signal detection;
- Quality Discovery + Quality Universe maintenance;
- Signal, Candidate, and Case registries;
- Theme Hypotheses and Experimental Theme tracking (supporting context);
- QAD candidate-first research queue;
- autonomous QAD discovery/selection within approved governance (M5+);
- full QAD research for candidates passing gates;
- human review;
- overrides;
- postmortems;
- approved knowledge;
- narrative export.

## Not in Scope

- broker sync;
- execution;
- automated allocation;
- autonomous investment recommendations or endorsement;
- final scoring weights;
- final technology stack;
- uncontrolled global universe expansion (bounded researchable universe policy governs);
- unauthorized portfolio-aware research;
- Capital Command integration (external system);
- Trading / Execution Systems (external system).
