# Product Vision

Build a global investment-intelligence system that reduces an enormous universe of information and assets into a small, evidence-backed, reviewable set of themes and candidates.

The system must help the Founder:

- detect meaningful change;
- discover themes early;
- distinguish hype from evidence progression;
- identify leaders, challengers, enablers, bottlenecks, and second-order beneficiaries;
- preserve counter-evidence and uncertainty;
- decide what deserves deeper research;
- learn from outcomes over time.

The platform supports decisions. It does not replace them.
