# Product Vision

Build a global investment-intelligence system that reduces an enormous universe of securities into a small, evidence-backed, reviewable set of **Quality-at-Dislocation (QAD)** candidates and research cases.

The system must help the Founder:

- observe broadly — every eligible company observable by the system; not every company reasoned about by an LLM;
- detect dislocation and quality signals through structured sensors and discovery intelligence;
- distinguish temporary impairment from structural damage;
- preserve counter-evidence, uncertainty, and decision history;
- decide what deserves deeper investigation;
- learn from outcomes over time without rewriting history.

The platform supports decisions. It does not replace them.

Autonomous QAD discovery and candidate selection is permitted within approved QAD governance. The platform never autonomously endorses investments, allocates capital, or executes trades.
