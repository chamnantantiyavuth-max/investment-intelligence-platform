## Proposed Changes

### §1 Mission (RETAIN + EXTEND)

**Proposed:**
> The Investment Intelligence Platform specializes in **Quality at Dislocation (QAD)** — identifying high-quality businesses experiencing material market and/or business dislocation and investigating whether the impairment is temporary, structural, or unresolved.
>
> It preserves evidence, uncertainty, disagreement, decision history, and dissent throughout.
>
> The central question is: *"Is this a high-quality business whose temporary economic impairment is being priced as permanent — or is the market correctly pricing permanent damage that the evidence has not yet confirmed?"*

It does not autonomously answer: *"What should be bought, sold, or allocated?"*

### §2 Product Structure (REPLACE)

**Proposed:**
> The product is organized as a QAD Research & Underwriting Institution. Its logical capabilities include Quality Discovery, Dislocation Detection, Autonomous Selection, Research Orchestration, Analytical Core (Business Quality, Industry, Financial, Management), Impairment Diagnosis, Normalization & Valuation, Structural Challenge, Research Audit, Underwriting Synthesis, Thesis Monitoring, and Knowledge Compounding.
>
> Legacy strategy authorities (Alpha Momentum, Close System, Fundamental & Opportunity, Institutional Intelligence) **cease to be co-equal investment paths**. Their data, methods, or capabilities may be retained or repurposed as supporting QAD intelligence where separately authorized.

### §5 Theme Intelligence (AMEND)

**Proposed:**
> In a QAD-specialized system, Theme Intelligence serves as a **supporting analytical capability**, not a mandatory research gateway. The research queue is candidate-first, driven by Quality Discovery and Dislocation Radar. Theme lifecycle concepts remain valid for contextual use.

### §13 Alpha Momentum (AMEND)

**Proposed:**
> Alpha Momentum is a **completed historical component**. Its pipeline and artifacts are preserved as frozen historical records. It carries no active strategic authority. Its data or methods may be retained as supporting QAD inputs where applicable.

### §15 Close System (AMEND)

**Proposed:**
> Close System is a **completed historical component**. Its pipeline and artifacts are preserved as frozen historical records. It carries no active strategic authority.

### §20 V0 Thesis (REPLACE)

**Proposed:**
> QAD V0 proves an end-to-end research workflow from Quality Discovery through Founder-ready research publication, using controlled historical and synthetic fixtures to validate source recall, citation correctness, calculation reproducibility, classification calibration, thesis-killer detection, and evidence recall.

### §24 — New: QAD Research Doctrine (NEW)

> **24.1 Quality at Dislocation.** The platform specializes in identifying quality businesses experiencing material dislocation. "Temporary" is an output of research, never an input assumption.
>
> **24.2 Autonomous Selection Boundaries.** The system may autonomously select candidates for Full Research through policy-driven Hard Gates. It may autonomously open research cases, conduct analysis, generate challenge, and produce Founder-ready publications. It may not autonomously endorse conclusions, allocate capital, or execute transactions.
>
> **24.3 Research Integrity.** Every material claim requires traceable evidence. Every material hypothesis must include a falsification condition. Independent challenge and audit are mandatory before Founder-ready publication.
>
> **24.4 Founder Authority.** The Founder retains final authority for investment judgment. Founder-Ready and Founder-Endorsed are separate states.
