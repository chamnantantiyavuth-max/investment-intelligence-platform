## Proposed Changes

### §1 Mission (RETAIN + EXTEND)

**Current:**
> The Investment Intelligence Platform exists to reduce the global investment search space while preserving evidence, uncertainty, disagreement, and decision history. It helps answer: "What deserves further investigation?"

**Proposed:**
> The Investment Intelligence Platform exists as a **Quality at Dislocation (QAD) research and underwriting institution**. It autonomously identifies high-quality businesses experiencing material dislocation, investigates whether the deterioration is temporary, mostly temporary, mixed, structural, or unresolved, reconstructs normalized economics, analyzes price-implied expectations, challenges the thesis through an independent structural Red Team, and produces rigorous Thai-language long-form research reports for Founder judgment.
>
> It preserves evidence, uncertainty, disagreement, decision history, and dissent throughout. Its central question is: *"Is this a high-quality business whose temporary economic impairment is being priced as permanent, or are we buying the past of a business that is structurally deteriorating?"*
>
> It does not autonomously answer: *"What should be bought, sold, or allocated?"*

### §2 Product Structure (REPLACE)

**Current:**
> The primary entry point is the Strategy Control Center, leading to: 1. Alpha Momentum, 2. Close System, 3. Future strategy modules. A Shared Intelligence Core supports all strategies.

**Proposed:**
> The product is organized as a QAD Research & Underwriting Institution with the following logical subsystems:
>
> 1. **Quality Discovery System** — Open-system quality universe maintenance
> 2. **Dislocation Radar** — Detection of material dislocations
> 3. **Autonomous Selection** — Hard Gates + Priority Ordering for case selection
> 4. **Case Orchestrator** — Research lifecycle management
> 5. **Analytical Core** — Business Quality, Industry Economics, Financial Reconstruction, Management Analysis
> 6. **Impairment Diagnosis** — Temporary/Mostly/Mixed/Structural/Unresolved classification
> 7. **Normalization & Valuation** — Normalized economics, DCF, Reverse DCF
> 8. **Structural Red Team** — Independent challenge
> 9. **Research Auditor** — Integrity verification
> 10. **Chief Underwriting** — Synthesis and Founder-ready publication
> 11. **Monitoring** — Thesis-aware monitoring
> 12. **Knowledge Compounding** — Cross-case learning
>
> Legacy strategy modules (Alpha Momentum, Close System, Fundamental & Opportunity, Institutional Intelligence) are retained as frozen historical components — read-only, no new development, no co-equal strategic authority.

### §5 Theme Intelligence (AMEND)

**Proposed addition:**
> In a QAD-specialized system, Theme Intelligence serves as a **supporting analytical capability**, not a mandatory research gateway. Themes may inform industry context, competitor analysis, and capital cycle assessment. The research queue is NOT theme-first — it is candidate-first, driven by Quality Discovery and Dislocation Radar. Theme lifecycle, Approval Status, and Monitoring Status remain valid concepts for legacy infrastructure and contextual use.

### §7 Weak Signal Inbox (AMEND)

**Proposed addition:**
> In the QAD context, the Weak Signal Inbox is superseded by the **Dislocation Radar** for anomaly detection. The Inbox remains available as a legacy observation point but is no longer the primary anomaly pathway.

### §13 Alpha Momentum (AMEND)

**Proposed addition:**
> Alpha Momentum is a **completed historical component**. Its pipeline, themes, candidates, and acceptance criteria are preserved as frozen artifacts for reference and regression testing. It carries no active strategic authority. No new Alpha Momentum development shall occur. Its frozen screens may serve as evidence inputs to QAD analysis where relevant.

### §15 Close System (AMEND)

**Proposed addition:**
> Close System is a **completed historical component**. Its Product Radar pipeline, synthetic fixtures, and display are preserved as frozen artifacts. It carries no active strategic authority. The Options Overlay remains deferred and abandoned. CS Product Discovery is frozen with the rest of Close System.

### §16 Learning Loop (AMEND)

**Proposed addition:**
> The QAD Learning Loop is revised as:
> 
> `Evidence → Hypothesis → Impairment Classification → Outcome → Postmortem → Candidate Lesson → Cross-Case Validation → Independent Review → Approved Knowledge → Industry Playbook`
>
> A single case conclusion does NOT become Approved Institutional Knowledge without cross-case validation and independent review.

### §20 V0 Thesis (REPLACE)

**Current:**
> V0 proves an end-to-end Alpha Momentum slice using controlled data and predefined themes.

**Proposed:**
> QAD V0 proves an end-to-end research workflow from Quality Discovery through Founder-ready Thai PDF publication, using controlled historical fixtures to validate source recall, citation correctness, claim support, calculation reproducibility, classification calibration, thesis-killer detection, and Decision-Changing Evidence Recall.
