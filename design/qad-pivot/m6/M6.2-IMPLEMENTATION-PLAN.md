## Deliverable

Construct one immutable, deterministic SEALED provider-input snapshot from an authoritative `PITC-01` context and a caller’s requested source IDs, using only canonical `SRC-01`, exact RawSourceArchive bytes, and valid archive-owned admission attestations. Reject the entire requested corpus if any authority, timing, identity, integrity, or byte-binding condition fails. The snapshot marks `closed_corpus_required=True`; it does not call a provider.

The deterministic `input_snapshot_hash` must match the approved M6.0 identity definition exactly: case ID, case version, PIT mode, AS_OF date, and the canonical ordered set of source IDs with their exact raw-blob SHA-256 values. Volatile admission-attestation IDs, creation time, object identity, storage location, and PIT-context record ID are not identity inputs. The M6.0 §9 / S7 rule also requires `SRC-01.publication_date` to exist and be no later than AS_OF for SEALED eligibility.

## Inherited invariants

- M6.1 archive-owned, immutable admission attestation remains atomic with SRC-01 and exact raw bytes; the caller cannot set or backdate `admitted_at`.
- `retrieval_date` is not proof of historical capture. `LEGACY_UNATTESTED_SRC01` and `SRCV_ONLY_CAPTURE_PROOF` remain SEALED-ineligible.
- SEALED eligibility requires every condition in M6.0 §5 (A–K), including admission no later than PITC AS_OF, matching source identity/content hash, exact blob SHA-256 and length, exact captured bytes, and immediate full revalidation before snapshot construction.
- Any source failure produces the policy outcome `UNAVAILABLE_FOR_SEALED_PIT` (with a specific diagnostic reason where available) and fails the whole request; no partial corpus, fallback to detached bytes, synthesized admission time, or live retrieval.
- Preserve M6.1 backdating, rollback, identity-binding, legacy/SRCV fail-closed behavior and persistence/M5.3 regressions.
- Do not modify canonical M4A schemas or change PIT, evidence, Research Room, or Run Ledger semantics.
