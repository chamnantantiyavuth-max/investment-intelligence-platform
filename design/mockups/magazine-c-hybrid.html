Company Research Note · JNJ · RM-2026-0003

JNJ's Talc Proposal: A Conditional Schedule, Not Finality

A $5.5B commitment that executes only if 95% of 76,000 claimants agree, first cash deferred to 2027, and the balance sheet has not yet accrued the plan.

Equity Research · Published Aug 7, 2026 · Advisory only · Portfolio-blind
Provenance: 8-K 7/28/26 · Real data · Point-in-time (FD #58)

The filing is a schedule, not a settlement. JNJ proposes to pay $5.5 billion to resolve the ovarian-talc claims — but the money moves only if participation clears 95% of the 76,000 claimants, the first tranche is capped at $3 billion and deferred to 2027, and the $3.7 billion reserve carried at Q2-26 predates the proposal entirely.
