Company Research Note · RM-2026-0003
JNJ's Talc Proposal: A Conditional Schedule, Not Finality

A $5.5B commitment that executes only if 95% of 76,000 claimants agree, first cash deferred to 2027, and the balance sheet has not yet accrued the plan.

Equity Research · Published Aug 7, 2026 · JNJ
Real data · 8-K 7/28/26 Portfolio-blind Advisory only

The filing is a schedule, not a settlement. JNJ proposes to pay $5.5 billion to resolve the ovarian-talc claims that have shadowed the company for a decade — but the money moves only if participation clears 95% of the 76,000 claimants.

"The clean-up exists only after participation, recognition, residual dockets, and capital commitments execute together." — CRO opposing essay

The first tranche is capped at $3 billion and deferred to 2027; the $3.7 billion reserve carried at Q2-26 predates the proposal entirely.
