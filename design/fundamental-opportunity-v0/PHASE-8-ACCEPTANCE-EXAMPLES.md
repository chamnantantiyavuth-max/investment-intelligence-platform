**When:** The system classifies AAPL's moat

**Then:**
- Moat Width = WIDE ✅

- Moat trend = STABLE (recorded for monitoring)

## Scenario 2: Narrow Moat, Cyclical Trap — Intel (INTC)

**Given:** INTC is flagged as "Unusually Cheap" — P/E 14x vs 5Y avg 22x (-2.1σ)

**When:** Value Trap Detector runs the 5 questions

**Value Trap Check:**
```
Q1: Earnings still growing? → ❌ Revenue -8% YoY, EPS -35%
Q2: Industry growing? → ❌ PC TAM flat, x86 share loss to ARM
Q3: Moat intact? → ❌ Process leadership lost to TSMC; Apple defected to M-series
Q4: Management credible? → ⚠️ 4th CEO in 6 years; missed foundry targets
Q5: Cheap for GOOD reason? → ✅ YES — structural decline, not cyclical dip
```

**Then:**
- Value Trap Score = 0/5 (Definite Value Trap) ✅
- Action = 🔴 Do NOT add to "Cheap & Quality" watchlist
- Evidence = Archived with trap classification
- Moat Assessment: Narrow (Share of Mind: Weak — brand declining), Shallow depth

## Scenario 3: Earnings Quality — Cosmetic Beat

**Given:** Company XYZ reports Q2 EPS of $1.20 vs consensus $1.00 (20% beat)

**When:** Earnings Quality dimension is assessed

**Digging Deeper:**
```
├── Revenue: -3% YoY (declining!)
├── EPS growth: All from $5B share buyback — share count reduced 4%
├── Operating income: -2% YoY
├── One-time items: $0.15 EPS from legal settlement
├── FCF/EPS ratio: 0.4x (accounting earnings, not cash)
└── Guidance: Unchanged (didn't raise despite "beat")
```

**Then:**
- Quality Rating = **COSMETIC** ✅
- Conviction Impact = Red flag — investigate before acting
- Thesis Impact = WEAKENS (declining revenue hidden by buybacks)
- Surprise Direction = BEAT (but cosmetic — not actionable)
