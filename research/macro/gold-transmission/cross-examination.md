## 1. Confirmed material-claims list (final)

- C1 — Core thesis: gold's observed resilience is consistent with temporary demand/risk-premium forces outweighing the usual real-yield drag; the evidence does not establish a permanent break.
- C2 — Traditional transmission: higher real yields raise gold's opportunity cost; the 47bp 10y-real rise should be a drag, all else equal.
- C3 — FOMC signal: the 9-3 hold (all dissents for +25bp) directionally reinforces the restrictive-policy/opportunity-cost signal.
- C4 — Residual attribution: the modest dollar decline is unlikely to explain the full gold increase; the unmatched residual cannot be uniquely attributed from this comparison.
- C5 — Geopolitical insurance: plausible but unquantified candidate channel.
- C6 — Inflation/debasement: elevated CPI makes inflation-risk hedging plausible; data do not measure gold demand or relative importance.
- C7 — Policy-error hedging: two-sided insurance possible; actual hedge demand not demonstrated.
- C8 — Official-sector absorption: possible channel; contribution unassessable without refreshed purchase data.
- C9 — Confirm/break tests: appropriate variables, subject to stronger identification design.
- C10 — Concluding interpretation: "current-regime flow dominance" = hypothesis consistent with the snapshot; "permanent break" not established.

## 3. Required note corrections

1. "Flows are currently overpowering/dominating" → "the observed configuration is consistent with a flow-dominance hypothesis".
2. Synchronize gold/dollar/yield horizons to a common start/end; until then retain the mismatched-horizon limitation prominently (thesis, residual, conclusion).
3. Label year-end comparators radar-attributed: 2y 3.47%, 10y nominal 4.18%, 10y real 1.93%.
4. Retain re-performed arithmetic: 4.20−3.47=73bp; 4.63−4.18=45bp; 2.40−1.93=47bp; $4,206.60÷1.246=$3,376.08.
5. "The unexplained residual must therefore lie mainly in other demand channels" → non-identifying residual statement (demand, supply, positioning, expectations, risk-premium).
6. Remove unsupported rankings: "leading candidate", "second-highest weight", "moderately persuasive" → plausible but unranked hypotheses.
7. CPI is not the Fed's target index (PCE); use CPI as inflation-persistence evidence, not like-for-like vs 2% objective.
8. FOMC: "strengthens the opportunity-cost case" → "directionally reinforces the restrictive-policy signal"; distinguish dissent preferences from enacted policy.
9. Delete the duplicated official-sector/lasting-transmission paragraph (dispatch copy artifact; the source file has it once — clean copy must not duplicate).
10. Confirmation framework: next real-yield move = diagnostic, not dispositive; multi-event/event-study design; pre-specified regression windows, significance thresholds; horizon-matched WGC/ETF/CFTC/dollar/official-purchase data.

## 5. Aggregate note on supporting statements

Supporting arithmetic internally correct ($3,376.08 prior-year gold; 73/45/47bp yield changes; dollar/CPI figures used consistently). The explanatory discussion is economically coherent but repeatedly moves from consistency to attribution without direct flow evidence, uses unmatched horizons, unsupported channel rankings, and one duplicated paragraph — retain as mechanism descriptions and limitations, not separately proven findings.

## 6. Three-sentence assessment

The note successfully rejects the strongest claim that one snapshot proves a permanent breakdown of gold's traditional real-yield transmission channel. It does not establish that conflict, inflation-risk, policy-error, or official-sector flows caused the resilience or that those flows can be ranked — the affirmative "flow dominance" thesis must be recast as a provisional interpretation. After horizon alignment, refreshed flow data, and a pre-specified multivariate or event-study test, the note could distinguish temporary regime dependence from omitted-variable co-movement more credibly.
