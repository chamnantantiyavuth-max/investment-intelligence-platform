## 1. Deferral-condition: the window opened

The 2026-08-07 deferral (Founder triage A) asked for a *settled macro window*: Hormuz was live,
the jobs report was pending, and the price was still moving. Since then both scheduled data events
have printed cleanly — **July NFP −23,000 with unemployment at 4.1% (released 8 Aug)** and
**July CPI +0.1% m/m / 3.4% y/y with core subdued (released 12 Aug)** — and the market has
reassessed the rate path around them (September-hike odds plunged; rate-cut bets intensified,
S11). The two clean prints give the multi-observation test the 0008 note said was required
("the next discrete rise in real yields is one diagnostic observation, not a clean confirmation
test... a credible test requires multiple events"). Hormuz is **not** settled — the premise has
inverted (S12–S14) — and that inversion is itself a finding (section 3). Verdict: **execute the
test**, with the residual Hormuz tail carried as a named limitation.

## 2. What the second observation shows (obs 2, 12 Aug)

Gold has extended the rally to **+11.01% from the 3 Aug close** (GC=F 4,033.70 → 4,477.90;
crossed $4,500 intraday in New York on 12 Aug — first time since June), while:

- **Real yields sit near cycle highs**: DFII10 2.43% on 10 Aug (peak 2.47% on 31 Jul; 8/7 2.40%).
  The concurrent inversion with the yield LEVEL persists at larger scale. (8/11–8/12 DFII10 not yet
  published by FRED at pull time — stated as unmeasured; nominal 10y ^TNX 4.68 on 8/12, −2bp.)
- **Equities remain at records**: S&P 500 7,751.42 (8/12), +0.36% 5d, risk-on sustained.
- **The geopolitical premise inverted**: the 7 Aug "Hormuz de-escalation" (WTI −11% from peak,
  84.67 → 75.22) proved premature. By 10–12 Aug the standoff is live — US–Iran deal in doubt, ship
  traffic near a three-month low, attacks denting reopening hopes, IEA warning of a deepening supply
  shortfall (S12–S14). WTI **+10.53% in 5 sessions** to 83.14, co-moving with gold.
- **The 11–12 Aug leg is explicitly rate-path driven**: gold's $4,500 breakout on 12 Aug was
  headline-attributed to tame CPI cooling Fed hike bets (Mining.com, S2); Kiplinger and Yahoo/Motley
  Fool report September-hike odds plunging post-CPI (S11); pre-CPI pricing had hike odds near 48%
  (IndexBox, S11) — a hawkish market that gold kept rallying into, then broke out as those odds fell.

## 3. Driver ranking at obs 2

**1. Monetary-policy expectations (rate-path repricing) — the marginal driver of the 11–12 Aug leg.**
The two macro prints (NFP −23k, CPI 3.4%/core subdued) cut September hike odds and intensified
rate-cut bets; gold broke $4,500 on the inflation print. This is the cleanest identifiable marginal
flow in the register, and it answers the card's question for the second observation: **yes — the
marginal driver rotated toward monetary-policy expectations**, at least for this leg. Critically,
this is the *traditional channel operating through expectations*: gold rose as the expected real-rate
path moved dovish. It does not require a structural break.

**2. Geopolitical premium (Hormuz) — active supporting bid, not the marginal driver of the 12 Aug leg.**
The premise inverted: gold and oil rose together (+5.5%/+10.5% 5d), consistent with a live
geopolitical risk premium. This partially dissolves the obs-1 anomaly: gold was not rallying
"against" geopolitical de-escalation on 12 Aug — de-escalation never durably materialized. The
7 Aug framing (gold up while geopolitics calms) rested on a premature read of the Hormuz deal talks.

**3. Official-sector flows — news-attested, unmeasured, unranked.**
Central-bank buying "holds through Fed rate uncertainty" (Crux, 11 Aug); China reported +20t (10 Aug).
Same status as 0008: candidate, not directly verified (no WGC horizon-matched data re-pulled).

**4. Dollar softness — mild, secondary.** DXY ~100 (flat 5d); broad dollar (DTWEXBGS) 119.06 on
8/7, softer than the 119.70 of 7/31. A tailwind, not the marginal flow.

## 4. The 0008 thesis at this scale

- **Flow-dominance (modest form): strengthened, not established.** The register now contains a
  second, larger observation of gold rising with realized real yields near cycle highs and equities
  at records — the concurrent inversion persists at +11%. But the mechanism is identifiable this
  time: rate-path repricing. "Consistent with flow dominance" is now better supported because a
  flow (rate expectations) is *observable* in the news attribution, though not yet *measured*
  (no ETF/CFTC/official data).
- **Permanent transmission break: NOT established — and this scale of move cuts against it.**
  If the channel were structurally broken, gold should rally regardless of the rate path. Instead,
  gold's marginal move followed the rate-path repricing — the *expected-real-rate channel operating
  normally*. The "break" appearance comes from measuring realized yield LEVELS (near cycle highs)
  against gold price action; gold responds to the marginal change in expected real rates, not the
  level. The level-vs-flow distinction from 0008 remains the correct reading.
- **What the 0008 confirmation framework said would weaken the strong interpretation**: "renewed
  negative sensitivity to real yields" — obs 2 shows exactly that sensitivity *through expectations*
  (gold up on falling hike odds), which is the traditional channel asserting itself, not breaking.

## 5. Conclusion (draft)

The marginal driver of gold rotated toward **monetary-policy expectations** in the second
observation: the 11–12 Aug leg was rate-path repricing after a weak NFP and a tame CPI, with the
geopolitical premium (Hormuz standoff — the 7 Aug de-escalation premise inverted) as a live
supporting bid and official-sector flows as an unmeasured candidate. The 0008 flow-dominance thesis
is **consistent with** the larger-scale evidence but the permanent-transmission-break claim is
**weakened**: the observed move is explained by the traditional channel operating through expected
real rates, with realized levels near cycle highs. No structural break is required, and none is
established. Limitation: ETF flows, CFTC positioning, WGC official-purchase data, and the 8/11–8/12
real-yield prints were not available at pull time — the flow ranking rests on price behavior,
macro prints, and news attribution, not on buyer-identity data. The next diagnostic: gold's response
to a *rise* in hike odds or realized real yields (a clean test of the break), and/or buyer-identity data.
