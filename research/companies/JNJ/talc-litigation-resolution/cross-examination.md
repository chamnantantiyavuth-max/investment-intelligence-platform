# Cross-Examination Memo — RM-2026-0003 (JNJ Talc)

## 1. Intent attribution outruns the record — **PARTIALLY SUSTAINED**

**Challenged text:** “The 28 July–4 August 2026 8-K cluster is **deliberate overhang compression**” and “JNJ moved the largest legal unknown in its history.”

**Observed:** JNJ announced a conditioned $5.5B proposal for 76,000 ovarian claims, with payments deferred into 2027 and later; it also announced Firefly/Sail transactions. **Scenario/inference:** that the cluster was orchestrated as one overhang-compression program. The talc release supports an intent to reach an “efficient conclusion” to ovarian litigation, but no source says the acquisitions, guidance update, officer transition, and talc proposal were coordinated for that purpose. “Largest legal unknown in its history” is unsourced ranking language. The economic mechanism—exchanging uncertain claim-by-claim exposure for a conditional payment framework—survives; the actor-level intent and cluster-wide coordination do not.

**Precise correction:** Replace the controlling thesis with: “The talc proposal is **consistent with an attempt** to compress part of the overhang into a conditional payment schedule; the contemporaneous capital actions increase deployment intensity but are not disclosed as a coordinated cleanup program.” Delete “largest … in its history” or source and define the comparison.

## 2. The $5.5B-versus-$3.7B treatment — **CHALLENGE REJECTED**

**Challenged text:** “The gap … (~$1.8B: $5.5B − $3.7B) is therefore a **comparison across different bases** … not a like-for-like shortfall.”

This is precisely the necessary warning. **Arithmetic:** $5.5B − $3.7B = $1.8B. **Observed:** $5.5B is a proposed nominal commitment with delayed payments; $3.7B is a June 28 present-value reserve covering executed settlements, defense, and other talc costs. The scopes, dates, discounting, timing, and accrual status differ. The essay appropriately refuses to call the difference an incremental charge or funding deficit. No correction is required, although “non-comparable difference” would be even safer than “gap.”

## 3. “Strongest legal position” adopts issuer significance — **CHALLENGE SUSTAINED**

**Challenged text:** “the MDL court’s July 22, 2026 show-cause order … gave JNJ **the strongest legal position it has held** in this litigation” and “The tort system was therefore the only remaining channel.”

**Observed:** the issuer release reports a show-cause order following withdrawal of specific-causation experts in two bellwethers; the 10-Q reports the motion was granted. Neither source establishes a longitudinal ranking of JNJ’s legal position, the likely disposition of all claims, appellate durability, or that every alternative resolution channel was legally unavailable. The essay converts a favorable procedural development into a superlative and exclusivity claim without the order, docket record, or independent legal analysis. That is issuer framing elevated into the controlling mechanism.

**Precise correction:** State: “The order appears procedurally favorable and may have increased JNJ’s settlement leverage, but the disclosed evidence does not rank it against prior positions or establish dismissal probability. After three failed bankruptcy efforts, JNJ pursued a tort-system settlement; the record does not prove it was the only legally available channel.” Cite the issuer characterization as such.

## 4. Capital sequencing is asserted, not demonstrated — **PARTIALLY SUSTAINED**

**Challenged text:** “aggressive, but sequenced and internally consistent,” “fund pipeline optionality … while legal cash obligations are pushed to 2027+,” and “each individually affordable.”

The essay identifies concurrency—litigation, pipeline, buybacks, and dividends—and acknowledges a higher productivity burden. It does not ignore interaction risk, but does not test it. **Observed:** $20.758B cash/securities, $49.036B debt, ~$8.7B estimated H1 FCF, $4.253B H1 repurchases, $1B Firefly cash, $785M Sail initial payments, possible $2.58B option payment, and up to $3B first talc payment in 2027. **Scenario:** continued FCF, distributions, option exercise, and settlement timing. No liquidity waterfall, debt-maturity schedule, minimum-cash assumption, or downside case supports “affordable,” “sequenced,” or “internally consistent.” Deferral creates overlap risk; it does not sequence obligations.

**Precise correction:** Retitle the section “aggressive deployment with retained option control but unresolved funding overlap,” remove “each individually affordable,” and present a dated scenario waterfall before making a capacity conclusion.

## 5. Change-conditions are only partly falsifiable — **CHALLENGE SUSTAINED**

**Challenged text:** “accrual … near $5.5B,” “residual … expense trending down,” “no new material filings,” and platforms “de-risk on clinical timelines that justify the deployed capital.”

Participation at ≥95% is observable. The remaining conditions are not operational: “near,” “materially different,” “economically meaningful,” “trending down,” “material,” “de-risk,” and “justify” lack thresholds, denominators, baseline dates, observation windows, and named disclosures. Channel-level legal expense may not be separately disclosed, so the proposed test may be unobservable from this evidence set. The accrual also need not equal nominal commitment because timing, scope, and accounting basis differ.

**Precise correction:** Define each test with a filed data field, formula, baseline, window, and decision rule; where JNJ does not disclose channel-level expense or holdout economics, label the condition **not observable from current disclosures** and use docket events/reserve roll-forwards only as imperfect proxies.

## 6. Calculation and citation controls — **PARTIALLY SUSTAINED**

**Challenged text:** “~43% faster” and the legal-position sentence cited to “[SRC-01; SRC-04].”

The $1.8B, midpoint cut, and 3,800-claim maximum show formulas. The buyback pace does not show its claim-site formula; add `(($4,253M/6) / ($5,953M/12)) − 1 = 42.89%`. Label it arithmetic, not forecast. “Up to ~3,800” also assumes exactly 76,000 claims and exactly 95% participation; preserve that conditional. No clear SRC-ID swap was found, but SRC-01 is an issuer release—not the court order—and cannot support “strongest.” The conclusion is appropriately conditional, yet the thesis/body retain definitive “deliberate,” “converts,” and “internally consistent” language that outruns the later admission that cleanup is merely “plausible and directionally supported.” Apply the corrections above to the thesis, section heading, and conclusion, not only the caveats.
