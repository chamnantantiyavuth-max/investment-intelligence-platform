## 6. Three-sentence assessment

The note successfully rebuts the mechanical inference that a 12.0% cash-paid decline equals a 12.0% reduction in current buyback intent, and demonstrates substantial ongoing Q3 execution. It does not yet prove that ASR settlement timing caused the nine-month decline — the FY25–FY26 cash/settlement bridge is absent and authorization capacity does not establish deployment pace. Defensible conclusion: timing is a plausible leading hypothesis, the buyback mask remained modestly operative, and durable deceleration remains unresolved rather than disproved.
