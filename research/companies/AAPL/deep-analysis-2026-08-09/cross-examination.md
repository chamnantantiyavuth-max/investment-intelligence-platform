## 1. One-control-point moat thesis — **SUSTAINED**

The double-count correction is valid, but “one core control point” over-corrects: Apple’s interface ownership integrates the system and concentrates rent capture, yet proprietary OS/silicon/security IP, brand demand, and supply-chain purchasing/outsourcing scale can preserve product differentiation or cost economics even if discovery and payments migrate above iOS [main essay §§2, 7; Apple FY2025 10-K evidence summarized in `evidence-log.md` §§3–5]. The controlling specification requires six moat types and says multiple types may compound; the essay’s table instead omits **Share of Mind** and substitutes **Migration Cost**, which is analytically useful but belongs under switching friction rather than replacing a required category [`project-definition/FUNDAMENTAL-OPPORTUNITY-INTELLIGENCE.md` §3.4.1]. Correct the thesis to “one primary integrator/control point plus independently testable complementary moat mechanisms,” while continuing to deny independent-evidence status to shared installed-base or Services-margin observations.

## 2. Earnings quality HIGH — **SUSTAINED**

The five-year record is broadly cash-backed, but the current controlling label outruns the latest annual evidence: FY25 net income rose **19.50%** while OCF fell **5.73%**, and FCF/NI fell to **88.18%** (`(111,482−12,715)/112,010`), below the specification’s stated HIGH criterion of FCF conversion above 1.0x [`evidence-log.md` §7; `FUNDAMENTAL-OPPORTUNITY-INTELLIGENCE.md` §3.5.1]. The net intangible balance nearly doubled from **$11.093B to $21.334B** (`+92.32%`) without a business-combination explanation, while working-capital movements, maintenance capex, and the nature of the additions remain undecomposed [`evidence-log.md` §§6d, 9; Data Steward view]. One anomalous year does not prove low quality, but it blocks HIGH: publish **ADEQUATE** (or formal-scale **MEDIUM**) until the next annual cash bridge and the capitalization/intangible roll-forward resolve the gap.

## 3. Value-trap INCONCLUSIVE — **REJECTED**

The challenge fails because the approved detector is conditional on an “Unusually Cheap” observation below **−2σ of the company’s own history**, and the evidence packet has no synchronized price/multiple series, defined lookback, mean, standard deviation, or current observation [`FUNDAMENTAL-OPPORTUNITY-INTELLIGENCE.md` §3.6.2; Red Team view §4; Data Steward view]. Current growth and margins lean against **realized operating erosion**, while regulation and AI make future rent erosion credible, but neither side supplies the missing valuation trigger. A directional “probably not a trap” verdict would bypass the specified gate; retain INCONCLUSIVE and discuss operating evidence separately.

## 4. Capital allocation A− — **SUSTAINED**

The evidence proves scale and balance-sheet capacity—not an A− quality grade: FY21–FY25 repurchases totaled about **$438.6B**, shares fell **10.1%**, and FY25 cash paid was **$90.7B**, but there is no synchronized repurchase-price-versus-intrinsic-value test and the FY25 cash/transaction-value bases differ because of settlement timing [`evidence-log.md` §§7, 9; buyback-mask main and opposing reports]. The approved specification expressly asks whether buybacks were value-accretive and what returns past acquisitions earned; the essay concedes both buyback-timing uncertainty and an insufficient M&A-return audit, and the $10.2B intangible increase remains economically unclassified [`FUNDAMENTAL-OPPORTUNITY-INTELLIGENCE.md` §3.4, Capital Allocation; main essay §5]. Because no approved letter-grade rubric exists, substituting B/B+ would invent precision; withdraw A− and state **strong capital-return capacity, allocation quality INCONCLUSIVE**.

## 5. “Retention with rent-capture loss” — **PARTIAL**

The essay does not literally bury the insight: it calls rent capture the controlling risk in the executive summary, labels the CRO insight in the value-trap section, and returns to AI/regulatory control-point migration in the change conditions [main essay Executive Summary, §§4, 7]. But it fails to give the mechanism controlling consequence: the core verdict still leads with “durable business, high-quality earnings” without saying that installed-base persistence and nominal Services growth are non-dispositive if profit captured per relationship deteriorates, exactly the CRO’s load-bearing challenge [CRO first-pass view; `reports/apple-moat-opposing-2026-08-06.md`]. Promote the mechanism into the core verdict and use it to organize the monitoring bridge—attachment/retention → transaction routing/take rate → Services gross profit → organic NI/OCF → buyback-supported EPS.

## 6. Services arithmetic and attribution — **PARTIAL**

The arithmetic is correct: FY25 Services gross profit share is `82,314 / 195,201 = 42.1688%`, rounding to **42.2%**, using FY25 Services gross profit and total gross profit from the FY2025 10-K; Q3 FY26 Services GM is `(30,739−7,494) / 30,739 = 75.6205%`, rounding to **75.62%**, from the Q3 FY2026 10-Q (accession `0000320193-26-000020`) [`evidence-log.md` §§1, 6; Services-margin verification report]. Attribution is complete at claim site for 42.2%, but “Quant view” is not adequate primary-source attribution for the exact 75.62% in §3; add the raw inputs, formula, accession, filing date, and **derived/reported-basis** label there. Preserve the existing boundary that unknown tariff-refund allocation prevents normalization and that gross margin is not category operating margin or free-cash-flow conversion [`reports/apple-services-margin-verification-2026-08-06.md`].
