# Internal Auditor / Red Team — Coverage Completeness

**Verdict: FAIL — not publication-ready.** The shared artifact remains fundamentally RM-2026-0001’s moat essay with adjacent financial observations, not a complete six-dimension deep analysis. Point-in-time evidence runs through the June 2026 quarter; conclusions must retain those dates and must not convert missing data into analyst-invented thresholds.

## Dimension verdicts

1. **Business model — PASS WITH CORRECTIONS.** Product/Services mix, geographic exposure, distribution, iPhone concentration, Services composition, margins, and the platform monetization mechanism are covered. Before publication, separate recurring/transactional Services economics where disclosure permits and explicitly state that aggregate Services reporting prevents attribution of growth or margin to App Store, advertising, cloud, payments, or licensing individually.

2. **Moat (six areas) — PASS WITH MAJOR CORRECTION.** All six required areas are named: Share of Mind, Switching Cost, Intangible Assets, Network Effect, Cost Advantage, and Efficient Scale. However, four apparent supports repeatedly derive from one ecosystem system: installed base plus integrated hardware/software/services can create switching friction, attract developers, spread fixed cost, and sustain Services economics. Treating each output as independent evidence would double-count one causal engine. Publish one core-system assessment, then identify only incremental evidence for each area; otherwise label the area an amplifier or unverified hypothesis. Scale and profitability are not themselves proof of entry deterrence or network effects.

3. **Earnings quality — FAIL.** The packet enables a starting review (five-year OCF/FCF, FY2024’s $10.2B one-time tax charge, Q3 tariff-refund benefit, and the Q3 working-capital contribution), but the moat essay does not provide a normalized earnings-to-cash bridge, accrual analysis, SBC treatment, maintenance-capex limitation, or recurring-versus-temporary margin reconciliation. Add a rerunnable annual and latest-period bridge.

4. **Value trap — FAIL / NOT CHECKABLE.** Spec 3.6.2’s required **−2σ historical range** cannot be tested from the shared evidence: there is no synchronized historical price/multiple series, defined lookback, mean, standard deviation, or current observation. Flag this as an evidence limitation and withhold a value-trap conclusion; do not invent a proxy threshold.

5. **Capital allocation — FAIL.** Buyback scale and share-count reduction are documented, but allocation quality is not. Add dividends, debt/cash posture, reinvestment/R&D, acquisition or intangible additions, and repurchase-price-versus-value discipline. Address how buybacks can preserve EPS while operating economics weaken.

6. **Valuation — FAIL.** No point-in-time market price, diluted equity value, normalized cash-flow base, method matrix, assumptions, sensitivity, or implied-return comparison is present. Moat durability cannot substitute for price paid.

## Publication blocker

Omitting the failed dimensions would misleadingly turn “durable business” into “attractive investment.” Also surface the Cook-to-Ternus transition, tariff-refund-distorted margin, regulatory monetization risk, and evidence aggregation limits in the controlling conclusion. Publish only after the four failed dimensions are completed or explicitly marked inconclusive with the missing evidence listed.
