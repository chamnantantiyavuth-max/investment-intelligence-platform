# Data Steward — Evidence Integrity

**Point-in-time:** 2026-08-09; FD #58 applies. Confidence rates evidence sufficiency, not AAPL’s attractiveness.

- **Business model — HIGH.** FY2025 10-K (accession **0000320193-25-000079**) supports product/service mix, geography, margins, revenue recognition, supply concentration and obligations. Q1/Q2/Q3 FY2026 10-Qs (**0000320193-26-000006**, **-000013**, **-000020**) and Q3 8-K (**-000018**) provide trend checks. Missing: product units, ARPU, service-level economics and a filed installed-base count. The 2.5B+ devices/1.5B+ subscriptions are management statements in third-party transcripts. **Must not claim** filing-verified installed-base size, service-level profitability or unit growth.

- **Moat — MEDIUM.** Filings show high Services margins, growing mix, deferred revenue, minority hardware share, competitive pressure, developer dependence and regulatory openings. IDC and Counterpoint indicate Q1 2026 share gains but disagree on #1: IDC has Samsung 21.2% versus Apple 21.0% (statistical tie); Counterpoint has Apple 21% versus Samsung 20%. Churn, retention, switching-cost, pricing-power, customer-surplus, teardown-cost and verified network-effect data are absent. **Must not claim** unqualified Q1 leadership, that margins prove moat strengthening, or that switching costs/network effects/cost advantage are quantified.

- **Earnings quality — MEDIUM-HIGH.** SEC XBRL and the 10-K support FY2021–25 OCF, capex, FCF, income and margins; interim filings support nine-month FY2026 cash flow. FY2024 net income includes a $10.2B Ireland tax charge; Q3 FY2026 gross margin includes about two points of tariff refunds; working-capital movements and maintenance capex remain undecomposed. **Must not claim** normalized reported margin, fully recurring owner earnings or assured cash conversion.

- **Value-trap risk — MEDIUM.** Filings document iPhone concentration, China exposure, supply/memory and AI-compute constraints, DMA/Epic/DOJ/Google risks and margin pressure; FY2026 filings also show strong growth. Quantified profit-at-risk bridges, recession stress evidence and probabilities are missing. **Must not claim** risks are immaterial, resolved or moat-breaking—or that recent growth disproves a value trap.

- **Capital allocation — MEDIUM.** Filings substantiate repurchases, dividends, share reduction, liquidity, debt and authorizations. DEF 14A is unextracted; acquisition returns, repurchase discipline and incentives are weakly evidenced. Reconcile $90.7B FY2025 cash payments with $89.3B transaction value/402M shares (settlement timing). **Must not claim** buybacks created value or occurred below intrinsic value.

- **Valuation — LOW.** History, shares and balance-sheet anchors exist, but no synchronized market price, lease schedule, dilution forecast, maintenance-capex estimate, discount rate, terminal growth, probabilities or comparator set. **Must not claim** intrinsic value, margin of safety, expected return or valuation-driven value-trap status.

**Prior corrections:** preserve corrected timestamps; source the €500M fine to Legal Proceedings (10-K Item 3/10-Q Item 1); distinguish buyback bases; show formulas for derived figures; retain calibrated margin, moat and stress language.

**Verdict:** overall confidence is **MEDIUM-HIGH** for operating/financial diagnosis, **MEDIUM** for moat and risk causality, and **LOW** for valuation conclusions.
