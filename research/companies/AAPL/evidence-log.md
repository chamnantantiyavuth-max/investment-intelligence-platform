## 3. Supply / concentration (10-K FY2025, Notes + Item 1A)

- Most components available from multiple sources; certain components from single/small number of sources (custom components not commonly used by competitors); new products often use custom components available from only ONE source
- Substantially all hardware manufactured by outsourcing partners, primarily China mainland, India, Japan, South Korea, Taiwan, Vietnam (geographic concentration — Cost Advantage dimension)
- Manufacturing purchase obligations $56.2B as of 9/27/25 ($55.4B within 12 months)
- Distribution: direct 40% / indirect 60% of FY25 net sales (Item 1)

## 4. Services narrative (10-K FY2025, Item 1 + MD&A)

- Services lines: Advertising (third-party licensing + own platforms), AppleCare, Cloud Services (iCloud), Digital Content (App Store, Arcade, Fitness+, Music, News+, TV+), Payment Services (Apple Card, Apple Pay)
- FY25 Services growth drivers (MD&A): **advertising, App Store, cloud services**
- Services revenue recognized over time; third-party app sales accounted net basis (only commission retained); Services net sales include amortization of deferred value of services bundled with products
- Installed base: 10-K does NOT publish the count. 8-K Q3 FY26 (CFO): "installed base of active devices reached a new all-time high across all major product categories and geographic segments" — qualitative confirmation of installed-base growth, no figure in filings

## 5. Item 1A Risk Factors — moat-relevant (10-K FY2025)

- **Competition:** markets "highly competitive"; aggressive price competition, downward pressure on gross margins; competitors with broad product lines, low-priced products, large installed bases; certain competitors can price "at little or no profit or even at a loss"; **Apple has minority market share in global smartphone, PC, tablet, wearables markets**
- **Concentration:** "the Company generates a significant portion of its net sales from a single product category [iPhone 50.4% FY25] and a decline in demand for that product could significantly impact net sales and gross margins"
- **Tariffs/trade:** US tariffs announced Q2 2025 on China, India, Japan, South Korea, Taiwan, Vietnam, EU + reciprocal measures; Section 232 semiconductor investigation; supply chain shift costly/time-consuming — direct pressure on Cost Advantage
- **Supply:** single/limited sources for critical components; industry-wide shortages; commodity pricing fluctuation
- **Regulatory:** antitrust, privacy/data localization, digital platforms, AI regulation; **DMA Article 5(4) €500M fine 2025-04-23 + cease-and-desist; Article 6(4) preliminary findings (fines up to 10% of net sales); DOJ suit; Epic 2025 Injunction (Ninth Circuit 2025-12-11, Supreme Court cert 2026-06-30); Google search-licensing remedies risk (10-K Item 3 / 10-Q Item 1 — Legal Proceedings)**; legal proceedings "generally increased over time"; patent claims on standards-enabled products
- **Margin:** "gross margins will be subject to volatility and downward pressure" (gross margin risk factor + MD&A)
