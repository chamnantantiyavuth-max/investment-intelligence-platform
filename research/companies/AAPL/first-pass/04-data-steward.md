# Data Steward — Independent First Pass (RM-2026-0001)

The evidence base is strong for Apple’s reported financial scale, mix, margins, cash generation, geographic exposure, and disclosed operating risks, but materially weaker for the behavioral mechanisms that constitute a moat. The highest-quality source is the FY2025 10-K (accession 0000320193-25-000079): its audited financial statements and notes directly support product and Services revenue, gross margins, R&D, cash flow, purchase obligations, and geographic results, while Item 1 and Item 1A provide management’s descriptions of the ecosystem and competitive risks. XBRL Company Facts is useful corroborating structured data, not a separate source of economic evidence. The Q3 FY2026 8-K earnings release (accession 0000320193-26-000018) is timely but furnished and unaudited; its figures are credible issuer disclosures, while statements such as the installed base reaching an “all-time high” remain unquantified management assertions.

The source inventory identifies all three FY2026 10-Qs, but they have not been extracted. That leaves potentially relevant interim footnotes, regulatory developments, contingencies, tariff detail, and balance-sheet movements outside the committed evidence set. No earnings-call transcripts have been pulled, and the optional DEF 14A is unlikely to transform the moat analysis. More important gaps are the absence of independent market-share data, customer or developer surveys, teardown and cost comparisons, retention data, and regulator-derived platform metrics.

For **Share of Mind**, the filings establish extraordinary commercial scale—FY2025 revenue of $416.2 billion, including $209.6 billion from iPhone—but sales are not direct evidence of brand salience or preference. There are no brand rankings, search trends, consideration surveys, loyalty measures, or willingness-to-pay evidence. This dimension is therefore supported only indirectly and should remain low-confidence despite the plausibility of the underlying proposition.

For **Network Effect**, the evidence is suggestive but insufficient. Services rose to $109.2 billion and 26.2% of FY2025 revenue, with a 75.4% gross margin, while management reported a record active-device installed base across categories and geographies in Q3 FY2026. Yet Apple discloses no installed-base count in these filings, active-user methodology, subscriber count, ARPU, churn, developer population, transaction volume, cross-device penetration, or evidence that each additional user increases value for other users. The observed pattern could reflect brand strength, switching friction, distribution, or bundled monetization rather than a true network effect.

For **High Switching Cost**, integrated hardware, operating systems, cloud, payments, content, and accessories create a credible mechanism, but the filings do not measure it. There are no retention cohorts, replacement rates, migration costs, multi-product attach rates, customer tenure, or switching surveys. Unit shipments have not been disclosed since FY2018, further preventing separation of unit retention, pricing, and mix. Switching cost is therefore a low-confidence qualitative hypothesis, not a filing-proven fact.

For **Cost Advantage**, the evidence is medium-confidence for Apple’s operating model but low-confidence relative to competitors. Outsourced manufacturing, $56.2 billion of manufacturing purchase obligations, low reported capital intensity, and substantial gross profit demonstrate procurement scale and a capital-light structure. They do not reveal per-device costs, supplier rebates, yields, logistics economics, semiconductor economics, or comparable competitor costs. Single-source custom components, geographic concentration, tariffs, and competitors able to operate at little or no profit are direct counterevidence to treating cost advantage as unqualified.

For **Intangible Assets**, the filings directly establish proprietary platforms, sustained R&D spending of $34.6 billion in FY2025, licensed and owned intellectual property, and a differentiated integrated offering. They do not quantify brand value, patent quality, developer preference, design leadership, or the durability of technological differentiation. Efficient Scale is similarly mixed: Apple’s revenue, R&D budget, distribution, and purchasing commitments prove enormous scale, but Apple itself reports minority global market shares and highly competitive markets. Scale is proven; an efficient-scale moat that deters viable entry is not.

Several data-quality adjustments are mandatory. FY2024 net income includes a one-time $10.2 billion net Ireland State Aid tax charge, so unadjusted net-income comparisons misstate operating progression. Q3 FY2026’s 50.1% gross margin includes approximately two percentage points from tariff refunds and should not be treated as a clean structural margin. XBRL contains repeated comparative facts from later filings; the sampled values are identical and can be deduplicated by period end and accession, but these are duplicate comparative contexts, not automatically “restatements.” Most importantly, the fiscal-calendar statement in the supplied context is incorrect: the 10-K says FY2025 and FY2024 each contained 52 weeks, while FY2023 contained 53 weeks. The source inventory’s reference to “Segment Note 18” also conflicts with the filing, which identifies Segment Information and Geographic Data as Note 13.

Over three to five years, genuine break evidence would include sustained ecosystem churn, declining active-device or cross-device penetration, developer disengagement, interoperability or regulation materially reducing platform control, AI interfaces commoditizing operating-system differentiation, persistent tariff or supply-chain pressure eroding normalized margins, or sustained loss of relevance in China and other major markets. The most decision-relevant additions are obtainable: FY2026 10-Qs and first-party call transcripts; IDC or Counterpoint share and shipment series; consumer retention and switching surveys; App Store developer and transaction evidence; regulatory disclosures; and product teardowns or BOM comparisons. Some will remain estimates or paywalled, but triangulating them would materially improve the case because the present filings verify outcomes far better than moat causality.

| Evidence cluster | Confidence | Basis and principal limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue, margins, cash flow, R&D, obligations | **High** | Direct 10-K/XBRL facts; accounting adjustments still required |
| Q3 FY2026 performance | **Medium-high** | Direct 8-K release, but unaudited and tariff-refund distorted |
| Services mix and growth | **Medium-high** | Direct values plus simple derivations; does not establish ecosystem causality |
| Installed-base growth / network effect | **Low-medium** | Qualitative issuer statement; no count, usage, churn, or two-sided metrics |
| High switching cost | **Low** | Plausible mechanism without direct retention or migration evidence |
| Relative cost advantage | **Low-medium** | Scale and outsourcing are proven; comparative unit economics are absent |
| Intangible assets / Share of Mind | **Low-medium** | Platforms and R&D are direct; brand preference and IP quality are unmeasured |
| Efficient Scale | **Medium for scale; low for moat** | Scale is directly observed, but deterrence of entry is not |
| Break conditions | **Low-medium** | Filing-supported risks, but thresholds and leading indicators remain unspecified |

The filings support a high-confidence conclusion that Apple has exceptional scale, profitability, ecosystem monetization, and operational reach, but they do not by themselves prove the durability of most moat mechanisms. Network effects, switching costs, and Share of Mind currently depend substantially on qualitative or undisclosed evidence, while Cost Advantage and Efficient Scale are only partially demonstrated. Overall confidence in the evidence quality is medium, with the greatest upgrade potential coming from quantified installed-base behavior, retention and switching data, developer economics, independent market share, and comparable cost evidence.
