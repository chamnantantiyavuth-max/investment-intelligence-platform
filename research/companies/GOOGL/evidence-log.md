## Draft findings (for cross-exam + audit)

- **F1 — Radar observation VERIFIED but materially incomplete.** The $25B 10-tranche IG notes (S1–S3) and $40B ATM (S4) are real and confirmed. But the radar card (8/10, pre-Q2-10Q analysis) framed the story as "~$65B in ~2 months" while MISSING the already-filed June 2026 equity raises: $30.5B common (incl. $10B Berkshire private placement) + $19.0B MCP (S6/S7). Actual H1 2026 gross financing: $105.8B (S8) — the single largest capital raise in Alphabet's history, by far.
- **F2 — Purpose: AI-capex funding is the EXPLICIT stated purpose.** The company's own words (10-Q + earnings release): proceeds from common + MCP "will be used for general corporate purposes, including capital expenditures to scale AI infrastructure and global compute" (S6/S7); notes "general corporate purposes, which may include the repayment of outstanding debt" (S3); ATM "primarily intended to be used to meet tax obligations associated with employee equity grants" (S5). The capex engine confirms the need: H1 capex $80.6B (+103%), Q2 FCF negative (S9). NOT primarily M&A (Wiz+Intersect $35.4B is one component but the stated purpose is capex). NOT buyback pre-funding — buybacks are at $0 (S8/S10), the opposite.
- **F3 — ATM drawdown: NONE as of 6/30/26, explicitly disclosed.** The Q2 10-Q (filed 7/23) already answers the radar's "visible only in the next 10-Q" question for the Q2 window: zero shares sold, full $40B available (S5). Next visibility: Q3 10-Q (filed ~Oct 2026). ATM purpose = employee equity-grant tax obligations, not AI capex — a tax/cash-management tool, not a funding-strain signal by itself.
- **F4 — Capital-structure read: MATERIAL SHIFT — equity issuance at a mega-cap with record cash.** Alphabet has historically funded via debt + cash flow (S14: FY2025 debt $64.6B, common issuance $0). In June 2026 it raised $49.6B of equity-linked capital (common + MCP) — its FIRST common equity issuance since IPO-era and a first-ever MCP — plus a $40B ATM, while debt face value doubled to $101B. This is a structural change: an FO-universe mega-cap with ~$240B cash+securities issuing dilutive equity (S10). The MCP structure (6.25% coupon, 2029 mandatory conversion at $355–$444/share) defers dilution and hedges via capped calls — sophisticated, signal of planned large long-dated cash commitments (AI infrastructure).
- **F5 — Canary status: PARTIALLY CONFIRMED.** The "canary for AI-capex funding strain" hypothesis has real support: Q2 FCF negative, capex annualizing +76% vs FY2025, debt doubled, first-ever common issuance. BUT: cash+securities GREW to $242.5B in H1 (S10) — this is opportunistic pre-funding / balance-sheet positioning (lock long-dated rates at 159–184bp over 10y; raise equity at $352–355 near highs), not distress. The Berkshire $10B private placement (S6) at a premium to the then-market is a strategic anchor-investor signal, not a fire sale. Verdict: strategic capital-structure modernization + AI-capex pre-funding, with genuine strain visible in FCF — both true at once.
- **F6 — Watch items:** (1) Q3 10-Q (~Oct 2026) — first ATM drawdown read for Q3; (2) Q3 earnings FCF sign (Q2 was −$5.9B); (3) FY2026 capex vs guidance; (4) MCP conversion price drift (stock at $344 vs $355–$444 conversion band → conversion economics favorable to holders at lower prices?); (5) buyback resumption timing ($69.5B authorization unused).
