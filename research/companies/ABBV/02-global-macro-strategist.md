## 3. Rate sensitivity of the franchise

- Dividend yield ~4% (with 50%+ payout intent) → the stock carries a yield bid; rate relief supports the multiple.
- Growth is volume/demand-led (immunology biologics, neuroscience), NOT commodity-price or rate-led — low beta to the macro cycle.
- FX: ~40% of revenue ex-US; operational growth (+9.5%) already strips FX. A weaker dollar (current regime) is a modest tailwind to reported USD growth.

## 4. What a report must not do

Do not frame the inflection as macro-driven. The macro window (rate relief) explains the MULTIPLE environment, not the EPS normalization. Mixing the two would be a category error.

**Core view:** benign macro tailwind (rate relief, weak dollar) supports the setup but does not drive it; the debt deal timing is opportunistic and worth one paragraph of context, nothing more.
