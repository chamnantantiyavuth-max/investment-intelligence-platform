# Equity Alpha Analyst — Independent View (ABBV)

**Date:** 2026-08-11 · **Mandate:** RM-2026-0005 (inflection deep research, from ORG-2026-0018)
**Sources:** E1 (8-K Ex-99.1, 2026-07-31), E2 (10-Q, 2026-08-03), E3 (companyfacts annual), H1 scanner record (universe-scan-2026-08-11.json)

## 1. What actually fired (and why it matters)

The scanner H1 fired: TTM diluted EPS $3.54 > prior 2y max $3.00 (as-of 2026-06-30). Revenue confirmed +10.2% YoY. Stage S2-early. **But the driver matters more than the trigger:** Q2 FY26 GAAP EPS $2.03 vs $0.52 (Q2 FY25) = +290% — and H1 FY26 net earnings $4,313M vs $2,230M = +93%. The comparable base was **artificially depressed**: FY2023–FY2025 net income ran $4.9B/$4.3B/$4.2B vs $11.8B in FY2022, dragged by acquired IPR&D ($1,035M H1 FY26 alone) and contingent-consideration fair-value swings ($3,905M H1 FY26, non-cash).

**Verdict on the trigger:** the inflection is REAL in the sense that reported earnings are normalizing toward the franchise's true earning power — but it is a LOW-BASE recovery + real growth, NOT a new earnings step-function. Adjusted EPS (+22.9%) is the honest run-rate signal.

## 2. Franchise quality — does the growth persist?

- **Immunology $8.786B (+15.1% reported / +14.6% operational) = 51.7% of revenue** — Skyrizi $5.505B (+~20% run-rate), Rinvoq $2.525B, Humira $756M (erosion ~complete, now <5% of portfolio). The Humira cliff is **absorbed**: FY2023 revenue bottomed at $54.3B and FY2025 rebounded to $61.2B.
- **Neuroscience $3.228B (+20.3%)** — Vraylar $1.071B, Botox Therapeutic $1.042B, Ubrelvy+Qulipta $742M, Vyalev $256M. Second growth engine, scaling.
- **Oncology −1.5%** (Venclexta $771M, Imbruvica $532M erosion, Elahere $211M) — declining but small (9.7% of revenue).
- **Aesthetics flat** (+0.3%; Botox Cosmetic $728M) — mature, no drag.
- **Apogee acquisition announced** (immunology, closes Q3) — guidance includes −$0.14 dilution; growth-accretive intent.

## 3. Earnings quality

GAAP net income is **noisy** (IPR&D + contingent consideration swings every quarter — $3.9B non-cash H1). The adjusted measure ($3.65 Q2, +22.9%) is management-defined and I cannot independently verify every add-back, but the cash-flow story corroborates: OCF ~$19B TTM, capex tiny (~$1B), dividends $11.7B/yr (grew every year), buyback only ~$10M/quarter (capital return is dividend-led, not buyback-led — so the EPS breakout is NOT buyback-engineered). This is a **high-quality cash generator with noisy reported GAAP**.

## 4. Value-trap / erosion check

Not a value trap on current evidence: growth is re-accelerating (+8.7% FY25 revenue), immunology franchise durable (Skyrizi/Rinvoq growth > Humira decline), pipeline (Apogee, Vyalev) reinvesting. Debt $70.6B is manageable at ~3.7x OCF — investment-grade, though the Aug 2026 debt offering (E4/E5) is a watch item (why raise debt with $5B cash?).

## 5. Stage / entry discipline (FD #89)

S2-early: price $247.97 > 50MA $240.71 > 150MA $222.39, 50MA +8.3%/mo — early, not extended. But the +290% GAAP print could attract momentum chasing; the fundamental question (is Adjusted EPS growth durable at 22.9%?) is what a note must answer, not the chart.

## 6. What a fair report must say

- The inflection is real but **low-base-augmented** — say it plainly.
- Growth durability rests on Skyrizi/Rinvoq/neuro — the data supports it, with the caveat that reported growth rates are "reported basis" (operational is ~0.7pp lower).
- Balance-sheet watch: debt offering Aug 2026 (why?) + Apogee integration risk.
- Stage discipline: S2-early is early — fine for research, not a reason to chase.

**Core view:** ABBV's inflection = real normalized-earnings recovery + durable immunology growth, delivered at an early stage. The honest framing is "low-base recovery with real franchise momentum", NOT "new step-change".
