# Global Macro Strategist — Independent View (LLY)

**Date:** 2026-08-11 · **Mandate:** RM-2026-0007

## 1. Macro relevance

LLY is a **secular-growth story with minimal macro beta** — incretin demand is demographic/medical, not rate/cycle-driven. The rate-relief window helps the multiple (growth stocks re-rate when discount rates fall), but the fundamental story would work in any macro regime.

## 2. What macro actually matters

- **Capacity economics:** LLY is building US/EU manufacturing (capex $5.26B H1) partly for IRA/price-policy resilience — domestic production insulates against tariff/policy risk
- **Drug pricing policy (IRA):** Medicare negotiation is a structural overhang on pharma; LLY's incretin franchise is large enough to absorb partial negotiation, but it's a policy tail-risk the report must name
- **FX:** non-US revenue share meaningful; weak dollar = modest tailwind

## 3. What a report must not do

Do not frame a 48% growth quarter as "macro beneficiary" — the story is company-specific (incretin demand). Macro sets the multiple, not the growth.

**Core view:** minimal macro dependence; the two macro-adjacent risks are IRA pricing policy and capacity/tariff economics — both secondary to the demand story.
