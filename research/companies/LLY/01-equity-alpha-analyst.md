## 3. The moat — GLP-1 franchise

- Mounjaro/Zepbound = category-defining; retatrutide (next-gen) 3 Phase 3 trials POSITIVE — the pipeline extends the franchise, not just defends it
- orforglipron (oral GLP-1) submission — expands addressable market

## 4. Risks (real, but different from ABBV/BMY)

- **Valuation:** at $1,231.94, P/E is high (TTM EPS $28.70 → ~43x; forward ~$36 → ~34x) — the multiple prices in perfection
- Competition: Novo (CagriSema), oral entrants — but LLY leading on volume
- Price pressure: "lower realized prices" noted for both Mounjaro US (+45% on 45% volume? — actually US revenue +45% with volume up more = price DOWN) — the volume story dominates
- Net debt rising (borrowing for capex/M&A)

## 5. Stage discipline

S2-early: $1,231.94 > 50MA > 150MA — but at 34x forward, "early stage" doesn't mean "cheap". The stage filter says the PRICE is early in its move; valuation says expectations are high.

**Core view:** The strongest fundamental story of the 4 (real hypergrowth + cash-confirmed + pipeline extending) but the most expensive. "Inflection" here is a growth continuation, not a recovery — and the question is valuation discipline, not earnings quality.
