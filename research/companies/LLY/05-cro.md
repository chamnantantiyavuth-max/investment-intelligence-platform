# CRO — Challenger View (LLY)

**Date:** 2026-08-11 · **Mandate:** RM-2026-0007

## The thesis to attack

"LLY = hypergrowth (48%) + best earnings quality (111% OCF conversion) + pipeline extending (retatrutide) — the strongest fundamental story of the 4."

## Challenge 1 — 48% growth is a CATCH-UP quarter, not a new run-rate

Q2 FY26 +48% vs Q2 FY25 $15,558M — but FY2025 was itself a supply-constrained year (Lilly couldn't make enough). The +60% volume is capacity coming online, not a step-change in demand. FY2026 guidance $85-87B = +30-33% — **management itself is guiding DECELERATION from 48% to ~31%**. The scanner's "+80.5%" and my peers' "hypergrowth" language flatters a normalization.

## Challenge 2 — 34x forward is pricing the NEXT two drugs

At 34x forward, the market already prices retatrutide approval + oral GLP-1 (orforglipron) + continued Mounjaro/Zepbound dominance. **The asymmetry is BAD:** if everything goes right, ~30% growth at 34x = fair; if retatrutide stumbles or Novo's CagriSema wins share, the multiple compresses faster than earnings grow. This is a great company at a price that leaves nothing to discover.

## Challenge 3 — Rising leverage in a rate-relief window

$5,284M ST borrowings + $8B LT debt in ONE quarter — LLY is funding capacity with debt. Fine at 48% growth; uncomfortable if growth normalizes to 31% AND rates re-price. The report must flag the leverage trajectory, not bury it.

## Challenge 4 — "Volume +60%, price −" cuts both ways

Volume-led growth is high quality — but "lower realized prices" on Mounjaro US (+45% revenue on presumably higher volume) hints at **IRA/rebate/payer pressure already hitting price**. If price keeps falling as volume normalizes, the +48% compound becomes +20-25% — a very different story at 34x.

## Challenge 5 — The stage filter is doing the OPPOSITE job here

S2-early says "price hasn't run far yet" — for ABBV/BMY that means "room to run". For LLY at 34x, it means the price is early in a move that's ALREADY expensive. The stage discipline question is: does an expensive stock in early stage-2 deserve research priority over the cheap ones?

## What the essay must do

1. Present the DECELERATION explicitly (48% → guidance 31%) — don't let "48%!" stand alone.
2. Show the valuation asymmetry (34x forward, what's already priced).
3. Flag rising leverage honestly.
4. Note price pressure (IRA/rebates) alongside volume strength.
5. NOT recommend — at this price the honest output is "great company, demanding price, watch the deceleration".

**CRO stance:** The fundamental quality is the best of the 4 — but the RESEARCH VALUE is the worst: at 34x, everything is already known. A note that says "great company, expensive, watch deceleration" is honest; a note that presents it as an inflection opportunity is a momentum-chase dressed as research.
