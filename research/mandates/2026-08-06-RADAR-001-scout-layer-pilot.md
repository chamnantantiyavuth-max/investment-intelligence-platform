# RADAR-001 — Scout/Radar Layer Pilot (2-week bounded)

**Status:** APPROVED — FD #71 (Option A execution, 6 Aug 2026)
**Date:** 2026-08-06
**Owner:** `org-radar-scout` (Radar Scout Principal) + Scanning Assistant
**Purpose:** validate the Task Idea Card flow — radar scans → cards → CoS D1 triage → research intake (kanban Inbox).

## Watchlist (pilot focus areas)

1. **Silver / commodities** — follow-ups on the published Silver product note watch items (supply-demand balance, physical premium/dislocation, AISC proximity).
2. **Apple moat-erosion watch items** — the published Apple report's watch list (AI disintermediation observables, regulatory opening, Services economics).
3. **Broad macro regime** — rates, USD, inflation prints, equity breadth/leadership, cross-asset divergences.

## Output contract

- **≥2–3 Task Idea Cards** this pass, each with the kanban schema fields: `research_question`, `domain`, `materiality` (M0–M4, advisory), `principal_owner` (suggestion), `evidence_standard` + observed-what (sourced + dated, point-in-time rule FD #58).
- **Radar Digest** at pilot end (via IC Secretary): top 3–5 ideas + what was scanned + what was deliberately ignored and why.
- Honest empty result allowed: "nothing worth a card this pass" (no artificial activity).

## Non-scope

No thesis/analysis/recommendation (raise questions only) · no buy/sell/valuation · no portfolio data (portfolio-blind, Constitution §23.8.1) · no CIW path (FD #44) · no cron/automation (FD-CIW-005 — manual scanning only).

## Success criteria (pilot end)

- Cards are triage-able at D1 (research_question + domain + materiality present, sourced observations).
- ≥1 card becomes a research intake item (Founder decision).
- Card flow exercised end-to-end at least once (card → Inbox → triage → queue).

## Review

End of 2 weeks (or Founder call). Decision: continue as standing cadence / adjust watchlist / expand.
