## Material Findings
1. Proposal §5 creates a new five-layer product structure and positions CIW as a fifth peer capability, while Constitution §§2–3, FD #1, FD #24, and `INVESTMENT-INTELLIGENCE-OPERATING-MODEL.md` §§3–5 establish the Strategy Control Center and two equal intelligence paths. CIW §11–12 also repeats approved Phase 8 Company Analysis, Earnings & Change, Valuation Context, Independent Challenge, and Fundamental Research Package responsibilities from `FUNDAMENTAL-OPPORTUNITY-INTELLIGENCE.md` §§3–5. This would silently demote Phase 8 to "triage" and replace approved topology rather than complement it. **[Constitutional Conflict | severity: CRITICAL]** Correction: define CIW as the deferred Deep Research Handoff/operational workflow within Fundamental & Opportunity Intelligence, not a new peer layer. Verification: a responsibility matrix must show one owner for each Phase 8/CIW responsibility and preserve the Strategy Control Center and dual-path model. *(Audit Q1, Q2, Q12–Q14)*

2. Proposal §§5.5, 11, and 25 describe an implementable deep-research capability and vertical slice, but FD #44, `PROJECT_STATE.md`, `AGENTS.md`, and the prior Council decision explicitly keep Phase 11 Deep Research Handoff deferred. The proposal's current non-authoritative disclaimer prevents an immediate breach, but approval of the consolidated Bible or FD-CIW-001 could be interpreted as reauthorization by implication. **[Constitutional Conflict | severity: CRITICAL]** Correction: state that conceptual CIW approval does not authorize Phase 11, the pilot, schemas, automation, or implementation; a separate named Founder Decision must explicitly supersede FD #44. Verification: the revised proposal, FD register, roadmap, AGENTS, and project state must all show the same authorization status. *(Audit Q14, Q20)*

3. Proposal DNA-017 changes "Global Observation, Controlled Screening Universe" to "Global Observation, Controlled Investable Scope" and deletes the approved V0 boundary of US-listed common stocks and suitable ADRs. Proposal §§1, 5.5, and 10 additionally allow Founder-commissioned company research without distinguishing non-investable research subjects from investable Candidates. This can silently expand the investable universe and weaken breadth-first queue discipline. **[Constitutional Conflict | severity: CRITICAL]** Correction: restore the exact controlled V0 universe and distinguish global observation/non-investable company research from admission to an investable Candidate workflow. Verification: every Candidate and Research Request must carry its governing universe/version and reject out-of-scope investable classification absent a separate amendment. *(Audit Q1, Q9, Q14)*

4. Proposal §§10.4, 12.3 Modules N–Q, and 15 introduce "Attractive Below Price," "Investable Candidate," fixed 20/30/40% margin-of-safety displays, maximum rational price, opportunity-cost comparison, and sell/reduce analytical categories. These can turn valuation into an implicit recommendation or veto, conflicting with FD #24, FD #40, and `FUNDAMENTAL-OPPORTUNITY-INTELLIGENCE.md` §3.6, where valuation is contextual, non-dominant, and non-veto. The proposal also lacks approved deterministic contracts for owner earnings, maintenance capex, discount rates, terminal assumptions, normalization, and required return. **[Directional Risk | severity: HIGH]** Correction: classify these outputs as advisory scenario analysis only; prohibit mechanical investment classification, rejection, promotion, or portfolio action; require separately approved deterministic calculation contracts before official use. Verification: tests must demonstrate that valuation outputs cannot independently change Candidate, thesis, or investment state. *(Audit Q2, Q9, Q17, Q19)*

5. Proposal §10.4 and §13.2 create new Research, Investment, Thesis, and Artifact states without mapping them to the approved Candidate Research State and Thesis Lifecycle in `CANDIDATE-AND-QUEUE-MODEL.md` §§3.3–3.3.3 or to the Founder Decision Gate in the Operating Model §9. Terms such as "Thesis Broken," "Published," "Current Authoritative," and "Investable Candidate" lack actor, transition, evidence, and approval rules. This creates ambiguous authority and competing official state machines. **[Governance Gap | severity: HIGH]** Correction: reuse the approved states or provide an explicit amendment/mapping table with transition owner, evidence requirement, review gate, audit fields, and prohibited AI/Cron transitions. Verification: transition tests must prove that AI and automation cannot create authoritative states. *(Audit Q3, Q4, Q7, Q9, Q19)*

6. Proposal §16.1 Class C allows automatic publication of "low-risk, pre-approved, non-judgmental" updates, but those terms and the permitted fields are undefined; §17.4 merely says material changes require review "under approved authority rules." A Cron job could therefore alter canonical truth while claiming the change is non-material. **[Automation Risk | severity: CRITICAL]** Correction: disable automated canonical analytical publication during the pilot; permit only an explicit Founder-approved allowlist of deterministic metadata fields that cannot alter evidence, thesis, valuation, confidence, classification, or narrative conclusions. Verification: negative tests must show that Class C cannot modify canonical analytical content or authoritative state, including through retries or partial updates. *(Audit Q3, Q4, Q7, Q16)*

7. Proposal §7 correctly separates facts, claims, interpretations, and judgments, but §8 defines no pilot-specific minimum source gate, duplicates government/regulatory sources across Tiers 1 and 3, and omits the production source-admission contract required by `SECURITY-AND-UNTRUSTED-CONTENT.md` §Data-Source Admission. Proposal §15's compact Research Result also does not require claim-level evidence references or calculation lineage. A polished report could therefore appear complete despite missing primary sources, derived-source duplication, licensing uncertainty, or unsupported summaries. **[Evidence Risk | severity: HIGH]** Correction: define one minimal pilot source gate, source-independence handling, permitted exceptions, real-source admission fields, and claim/calculation lineage in the structured result; do not create a larger source ontology. Verification: a source-coverage report must mark missing/failed/conflicting sources and force an Incomplete or Review Required state rather than a complete publication. *(Audit Q5, Q6, Q16, Q17)*

8. Proposal §§13, 17, and 18 preserve versions and use an append-first update package, but they do not define the minimum evidence needed to change an official thesis, valuation, classification, or Current Authoritative paper. Nor do artifact-state transitions require the prior state, actor, reason, evidence, timestamp, and workflow version required elsewhere in approved governance. This makes the paper lifecycle only partially auditable and permits persuasive judgment to substitute for a governed transition. **[Governance Gap | severity: HIGH]** Correction: allow any relevant evidence to open review, but require a source-grounded update package, rerun deterministic calculations, visible counterevidence, independent review, and Founder approval during the pilot before official thesis/valuation/paper changes; "Thesis Broken" must satisfy a predeclared invalidation condition or explicit Founder decision. Verification: replay one earnings update from raw sources and confirm the old paper, proposed changes, review, approval, and new authoritative version are all reconstructable. *(Audit Q7, Q19)*

9. Proposal §20.1 says executor/reviewer separation applies only "when practical." Separate sessions or agents can still share the same framing, summary, unsupported assumptions, or generated conclusions. For high-impact company research, this does not make Independent Challenge genuinely independent and can reinforce persuasive but low-quality analysis. **[Research Quality Risk | severity: HIGH]** Correction: for the pilot, prohibit self-review; require a separate context, direct inspection of cited sources and calculations, an independence/provenance disclosure, and publication blocking when an eligible reviewer is unavailable. Verification: the review artifact must identify different executor/reviewer identities or contexts and contain source-grounded findings not copied solely from the writer's summary. *(Audit Q17, Q18)*

10. Proposal §25 calls the pilot "minimal," but one slice includes full research, independent challenge, Founder review, monitoring design, real or simulated earnings detection, update publication, a second independent review, Obsidian publication, structured return, and reflection. Combined with Modules A–Q and the multi-document target in §24, this tests several systems at once and conflicts with DNA-015 simplicity. One company limits breadth but not workflow complexity. **[Overengineering Risk | severity: HIGH]** Correction: first validate Approved Request → Source Map → bounded initial research → independent challenge → Founder review → structured result; defer earnings automation, recurring scheduling, canonical auto-publication, Obsidian synchronization, and the expanded file tree to later slices. Modules must be applicability-based, with justified omissions, rather than a mandatory long-report checklist. Verification: the first slice must be completable without Cron, a new repository/profile, a database migration, or an earnings-update subsystem. *(Audit Q10, Q11, Q15, Q20)*

11. The proposal combines constitutional mission and authority, amended DNA, domain topology, source policy, a detailed investment methodology, automation operations, artifact lifecycle, and target file structure into one replacement candidate. It omits or weakens approved provisions such as the exact DNA-017 universe, Constitution §8's three-year staleness default, §23.3 function classification, and detailed approved domain-state rules. Approval as a wholesale "consolidated Bible" could therefore cancel higher-authority requirements by omission or create duplicate governance truth. **[Constitutional Conflict | severity: HIGH]** Correction: retain the existing authority hierarchy and issue an explicit clause-level amendment/addition map; place mission, authority, external boundaries, and AI authority in constitutional governance, while source tiers, A–Q methodology, artifact workflows, automation registry, and file layout remain subordinate operational/domain documents. Verification: every changed or omitted approved clause must be marked Retained, Amended, Superseded, or Unchanged with exact authority and Founder approval. *(Audit Q12–Q15)*

## Required Changes
1. Recast CIW as a Phase 11 Deep Research Handoff workflow within the approved Fundamental & Opportunity Intelligence path; remove its status as a fifth peer product layer.
2. Add an explicit FD #44 non-authorization clause: approving the concept or methodology does not authorize implementation, automation, schemas, repository changes, or a pilot.
3. Restore the exact US-listed common-stock/suitable-ADR investable boundary and distinguish global observation or non-investable research from Candidate eligibility.
4. Reuse or explicitly map all proposed Research, Thesis, Investment, and Artifact states to approved domain states, including transition authority and audit fields.
5. Preserve valuation as contextual, non-dominant, and non-veto; treat margin-of-safety and opportunity-cost outputs as advisory scenarios pending approved deterministic calculation contracts.
6. Disable Class C canonical analytical publication for the pilot; allow only an explicit deterministic metadata allowlist.
7. Add the minimal pilot source gate, real-source admission contract, claim/calculation lineage, source-failure behavior, and minimum evidence rule for official thesis or paper changes.
8. Make independent review mandatory and operationally independent for pilot publication.
9. Reduce the first slice to initial research and structured handoff; defer earnings automation, recurring schedules, Obsidian synchronization, and expanded file structure.
10. Produce a clause-level amendment map. At minimum, assess amendments to `01-PROJECT-DNA.md`, `operational/FOUNDERS-DECISIONS.md`, `project-definition/DOMAIN-ARCHITECTURE.md`, `INVESTMENT-INTELLIGENCE-OPERATING-MODEL.md`, `FUNDAMENTAL-OPPORTUNITY-INTELLIGENCE.md`, `CANDIDATE-AND-QUEUE-MODEL.md`, `EVIDENCE-MODEL.md`, `operational/ROADMAP.md`, `SCOPE-AND-NON-SCOPE.md`, `DEFERRED-DECISIONS.md`, `OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`, `DOMAIN-GLOSSARY.md`, and the operational index. Avoid a constitutional amendment unless the Founder intentionally changes product topology or authority.

## Founder Decisions Required
- **FD-CIW-001 — Capability Boundary:** Recommend **approve in principle only** as a bounded Phase 11 workflow inside Fundamental & Opportunity Intelligence. This approval must not authorize implementation or supersede FD #44.
- **FD-CIW-002 — Execution Boundary:** Recommend **concept-only until the revised vertical-slice design is approved**. If implementation is later authorized, start as a bounded module in the existing repository; do not create a sibling repository or separate Hermes profile without evidence of a real context or permission need.
- **FD-CIW-003 — Framework Authority:** Recommend **research methodology**, subordinate to the Constitution, Evidence Doctrine, and approved Phase 8 specification—not an operational standard yet. Promotion to an operational standard should follow a successful pilot and approval of deterministic financial contracts.
- **FD-CIW-004 — Publication Authority:** Recommend **Founder review for every canonical analytical, thesis, valuation, confidence, and classification change during the pilot**. Only explicitly allowlisted deterministic metadata may update automatically.
- **FD-CIW-005 — Cron Authority:** Recommend **yes for observation and draft generation only**, with registered scope, idempotence, failure semantics, and no official promotion. Cron implementation still requires separate Phase 11 authorization.
- **FD-CIW-006 — Obsidian Role:** Recommend **confirm** that Obsidian is a narrative knowledge layer and never the sole source of structured official state.
- **FD-CIW-007 — Initial Pilot:** Recommend **defer company selection** until the capability boundary, source gate, methodology authority, and pilot scope are approved. Then select one source-rich company inside the controlled US-listed common-stock/suitable-ADR universe, without supplying portfolio data.
- **Phase 11 Authorization:** Recommend **remain deferred for now**. After the required corrections, the Founder should issue a separate, exact decision either continuing FD #44's deferral or explicitly authorizing the reduced pilot.

Treating that framework as an operational standard too early could quietly turn IIP from pluralistic, breadth-first intelligence into a company-first value-investing doctrine.
