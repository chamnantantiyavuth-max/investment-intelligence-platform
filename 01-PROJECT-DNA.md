# Project DNA

## Version 0.1

These are the non-technical traits that every future design, workflow, interface, agent, and implementation must preserve.

## DNA-001 — Decision Intelligence, Not Automated Decision-Making

The platform narrows the search space and improves reasoning. It does not autonomously decide what to buy, sell, or allocate.

## DNA-002 — Evidence First

Evidence is a foundational domain object. Hypotheses, themes, candidates, decisions, and lessons must remain traceable to their supporting and contradicting evidence.

## DNA-003 — Information Preservation

Do not compress meaningful complexity too early. Preserve uncertainty, disagreement, missing data, confidence, lifecycle, and alternative explanations before producing rankings or summaries.

## DNA-004 — Breadth Before Depth

Search broadly first. Deep research is applied only after the platform has reduced the universe to a small, high-value queue.

## DNA-005 — Shared Intelligence, Bounded Strategies

Theme Intelligence, evidence, entity mapping, and data-quality capabilities belong to the shared core. Alpha Momentum and Close System own only their strategy-specific logic.

## DNA-006 — Discovery Is Hybrid

Theme Intelligence must combine:

- top-down and bottom-up discovery;
- event-driven and change-driven detection;
- discovery of new themes and tracking of known themes.

## DNA-007 — AI Discovery, Human Approval

AI may create and monitor Experimental Themes. Only human-approved themes may affect official strategy outputs.

## DNA-008 — Theme Is a Relationship Structure

A theme is not merely a label or folder. It is an evolving network of drivers, evidence, industries, companies, assets, supply-chain roles, and market behavior.

This principle does not require a graph database in V0.

## DNA-009 — Every Material Hypothesis Is Falsifiable

Every important hypothesis must include supporting evidence, contradicting evidence, missing evidence, alternative explanations, confirmation milestones, invalidation conditions, and what would change our mind.

## DNA-010 — Human Authority Preserves Dissent

The Founder may override system assessments, but the original assessment and unresolved counter-evidence must remain visible.

## DNA-011 — Learning Never Rewrites History

Corrections, revised confidence, lifecycle changes, overrides, and lessons must be appended with history rather than silently replacing prior states.

## DNA-012 — Controlled Learning

AI may propose lessons and rule changes. Only human-approved changes may alter official rules, scores, thresholds, or workflows.

## DNA-013 — Everything Material Is Versioned

Data transformations, features, rules, scores, prompts, model outputs, approvals, and amendments must be versioned when they affect reproducibility or decision history.

## DNA-014 — Replaceable Intelligence Components

No LLM provider or agent framework may become inseparable from the domain model or historical record.

## DNA-015 — Simplicity Before Infrastructure Prestige

Use the simplest architecture that preserves domain boundaries, auditability, reproducibility, and controlled evolution. Avoid premature microservices, graph databases, autonomous agent fleets, or complex orchestration.

## DNA-016 — Honest Empty States

The platform must be able to return zero high-priority candidates and admit when it lacks a credible explanation.

## DNA-017 — Global Observation, Controlled Screening Universe

Global evidence may form themes. V0 investable screening remains intentionally bounded to US-listed common stocks and suitable ADRs.

## DNA-018 — Structured Source of Truth, Narrative Learning Layer

The application stores structured evidence, state, lineage, decisions, and outcomes. Obsidian and NotebookLM support narrative learning, case studies, playbooks, and reflection.

## DNA-019 — Deep Research Must Earn Its Cost

A long report is not proof of deep research. Deep research must be justified by expected decision value, uncertainty reduction, strategic relevance, or learning value. CIW research requests carry bounded scope and justified module omissions accordingly.

## DNA-020 — Company Research Must Return Intelligence to the System

Company research must not become an isolated paper archive. It must return structured findings to Candidate Intelligence, Theme Intelligence, industry maps, evidence progression, coverage-gap detection, and learning/postmortems. The CIW Research Result contract operationalizes this.
